# 🍔 The Suspicious Cookout Menu (Optional & Fun!)

Food is half the fun of a mystery dinner party! Here's a kid-friendly 4th of
July spread with silly "sleuth" names. None of it is required — serve as much
or as little as you like. Mix in whatever your crew loves.

> ⚠️ Grown-up note: check for **allergies** (nuts, dairy, gluten) before
> serving, and keep little kids away from the grill and sparklers.

---

## 🕵️ "Prime Suspect" Snacks (put these out as guests arrive)
- **Red-Handed Veggies & Dip** — red peppers, cherry tomatoes, and ranch. (Did
  someone get caught *red-handed*?)
- **Case-Cracker Crackers & Cheese** — a cheese-and-cracker plate.
- **Suspicious Chips & Salsa** — red salsa, "blue" corn chips, white dip = red,
  white & blue!

## 🍔 "Main Case" Grill Menu
- **Guilty Grill Burgers** — classic cheeseburgers.
- **Hot Diggity Dogs** — hot dogs (the "usual suspects").
- **Corn on the Clue-b** — grilled corn on the cob.
- **Watermelon Evidence** — juicy watermelon slices.

## 🎇 Red, White & Blue Treats
- **Firecracker Fruit Skewers** — strawberry 🍓 + banana or marshmallow ⚪ +
  blueberry 🫐, in that order on a stick = a mini flag!
- **Star-Spangled Popsicles** — red, white, and blue ice pops.
- **Sparkler Rice-Krispie Stars** — cut rice-cereal treats into star shapes with
  a cookie cutter.

## 🥤 Sip-and-Sleuth Drinks
- **Lily's Lemonade** — lemonade, of course! (Ask Lily if it's watered down 😉)
- **Sleuth's Blue Punch** — blue sports drink or blue lemonade.
- **Red Berry Fizz** — cranberry or fruit punch with a splash of sparkling water.

## 🥧 The Star of the Show: DESSERT
- **The Rescued Blue-Ribbon Apple Pie** 🥧 — serve the "stolen" pie AFTER the
  big reveal, once the sleuths have saved it! Tie a blue ribbon around the
  dish. Ice cream on the side makes it a champion.
- **Berry "Case Closed" Shortcakes** — biscuits + strawberries + whipped cream.

---

### 🎈 A few easy extras that make it feel like an event
- A **red-white-and-blue tablecloth** or paper plates.
- A little **American flag** as the centerpiece (and the thing everyone salutes
  during the crime!).
- A **"CRIME SCENE" sign** taped near the empty dessert-table spot (dollar-store
  caution tape is a fun touch).
- **Sparklers** for after the reveal — with a grown-up, once it's dark. 🎆

Have a delicious, mysterious 4th of July! 🇺🇸🕵️
