# 📣 THE GRAND REVEAL — read this aloud in Round 5

> **Detectives... the thief who stole Grandma Rose's blue-ribbon pie is...**
>
> 🥁 _(drumroll — have everyone pat their knees!)_ 🥁
>
> # 🏆 AUNT BELLA! 🏆
>
> "That's right — it was **Aunt Bella**, the neighbor who comes in SECOND place
> at the pie contest every single year!"

---

## 🔎 How the clues pointed to her (read this too!)

> "Here's how the whole thing fits together, detectives:
>
> **WHY she did it (the motive):** Aunt Bella has lost to Grandma Rose TWELVE
> years in a row. She was desperate to finally win the blue ribbon. With
> Grandma's champion pie gone, Bella's pie would win first place! That crumpled
> note — *'This is finally MY year... I'm so tired of second place'* — was
> written by Bella. (That's why the handwriting was fancy and loopy!)
>
> **HOW she did it (the plan):** Bella brought her own empty **pie carrier** to
> the cookout on purpose — Grill Master Gus even saw her tuck it behind the
> cooler, and she borrowed his **oven mitts**. During the **flag salute**, when
> everyone faced the flag and Uncle Sunny led the anthem, Bella slipped to the
> dessert table, grabbed the warm pie with the mitts, hid it in her carrier,
> and stashed it under her **lawn chair**.
>
> **How she got CAUGHT (the evidence):**
>   • 🍎 The pie dripped a sticky apple-cinnamon trail leading right to her lawn
>     chair — with a fork of fresh pie beside it (Clue 3).
>   • 👐 Ruby, hiding under the dessert table, saw the thief's hands were
>     covered in gooey apple filling — Bella's hands were a sticky mess!
>   • 🍋 Bella claimed she was 'in the kitchen getting lemonade,' but Lily was
>     at her lemonade stand by the kitchen door the whole time and saw NOBODY go
>     in. Bella's alibi was a fib!
>   • 🕵️ And she came prepared with a pie carrier and oven mitts (Clue 1)."

---

## 🚫 Why it was NOT the others (the red herrings)

> - **Grandma Rose** — It's her own pie; she wanted it back! She was up front
>   singing during the salute.
> - **Grill Master Gus** — He was at the grill the whole time; he's the one who
>   saw Bella's pie carrier.
> - **Coach Diego** — He handles all the pies (looks suspicious!), but he was
>   flipping burgers with Gus during the salute. Cleared!
> - **Ruby** — She was under the table eating cupcakes — that's how she became
>   our eyewitness.
> - **Lily** — Busy at her lemonade stand in plain view; she busted the fake
>   alibi.
> - **Uncle Marty** — Napping in the hammock; he saw someone hurry to the lawn
>   chairs but couldn't have done it himself.
> - **Uncle Sunny** — Leading the anthem in front of everyone — the best alibi
>   at the party.

---

## 🎬 What happens next

> **Aunt Bella (the player) now gives a big, dramatic CONFESSION!** Their

> me! Twelve years of second place — I just wanted ONE blue ribbon!"*
>
> Then **go retrieve the pie from under the lawn chair** (or wherever you hid
> your prop pie) — the blue ribbon is SAVED, and everyone can have a slice! 🥧
