# 📣 THE GRAND REVEAL — read this aloud at the end

> **"Neighbors, family, sleuths — every clue you needed was here all afternoon.
> A farm that nearly slipped away. A gold ring pawned in the spring. A bandaged
> thumb. A quiet figure slipping toward the barn during the salute. And a pie that
> someone couldn't dare let anyone cut open. Put them together, and they point to
> just one person...**
>
> 🥁 *(drumroll — everybody pat your knees!)* 🥁
>
> # 🚜 UNCLE PETE 🚜
>
> "That's right. It was **Uncle Pete** — the one person nobody ever suspects. But
> before you gasp... you need to know **WHY.**"

---

## 💔 WHY HE DID IT — the twist that changes everything

> "This spring, **Maple Farm was about to be lost.** Months behind on the taxes.
> The bank —" *(nod to Banker Bill)* "— was ready to foreclose. This farm, the
> home Grandpa Joe built with his own hands, the place this whole family gathers
> every year... was slipping away.
>
> So Pete did the only thing he could think of. He quietly took **Grandpa's real
> gold wedding ring** from the farmhouse desk, carried it to Pearl's pawn shop, and
> **sold it to make the payment and save the farm.** He slipped a cheap dime-store
> ring into the box, meaning to buy the real one back before anyone ever knew. He
> told no one. He carried it alone — to save your home, and to spare Grandma the
> heartbreak of learning her husband's ring was gone.
>
> **It was never about the pie.**
>
> Then this morning, Grandma announced she'd baked 'Grandpa's ring' right into her
> prize pie to give away at the fireworks. Pete froze. Because he knew: the moment
> that pie is cut open, everyone sees the ring is a worthless fake — and the whole
> secret comes tumbling out. So during the flag salute, he slipped away, took the
> pie, and hid it in the barn under a tarp. **Not to steal dessert — to buy time.**"

---

## 🔎 HOW THE CLUES POINTED TO HIM (read this too)

> "Here's how it all fits — and why no ONE clue was enough. Pete's tells were each
> shared by someone innocent, so you had to stack them ALL:
>
>   • 💍 **Pawnshop Pearl** saw Pete pawn a man's gold ring this spring — and
>     that ring is *still in her case.* (Banker Bill knew its worth, Charlie asked
>     about it, Mike found the pawn ticket — but only Pete actually pawned it.)
>   • 🩹 **A bandaged thumb.** Ruby, hiding under the dessert table, saw the
>     thief's hands — one thumb wrapped in white. Nurse Nancy bandaged **Pete's**
>     cut thumb that very morning. (Cousin Charlie had a bandage too — but Charlie
>     was holding the flag up front the whole salute.)
>   • 🚪 **He slipped away during the salute.** Lily, at her lemonade stand, saw
>     Pete leave the crowd and head toward the barn. (Lily slipped off too — but
>     only to the spigot, and right back.)
>   • 🚜 **The pie went to the barn.** Max saw a covered, pie-sized dish carried
>     into the barn and tucked under a tarp during the salute. (Gus and Fran were
>     at the barn too — but in the MORNING, not during the salute.)
>   • 🗄️ **Access.** Grandpa's ring lived in the farmhouse desk that **Pete** uses
>     for the farm's books. (Aunt Sunny had access while sorting Grandpa's things —
>     but she was leading the anthem up front when the pie vanished.)
>
> Motive + the pawned ring + the bandaged thumb + slipping to the barn + access —
> Uncle Pete is the ONLY person all five point to at once."

---

## 🚫 WHY IT WASN'T THE OTHERS (the red herrings)

> Each suspect had a real motive — but each fails at least one link:
> - **Grandma Rose** — it's her own pie; she wants it back.
> - **Aunt Sunny** — access & farm debt, but led the anthem up front (seen by all).
> - **Aunt Bella** — wanted to win & needed money, but bakes peach and was at her
>   table (Diego saw her).
> - **Uncle Marty** — pie-obsessed & in the barn, but snoring in the hammock during
>   the salute (Nancy saw him).
> - **Cousin Charlie** — debt & a bandage, but held the flag up front the whole time.
> - **Coach Diego** — handled every pie & bet on Bella, but at the grill with Gus.
> - **Grill Master Gus** — feud & a covered dish to the barn, but that was the
>   MORNING (brisket), and he was at the grill during the salute.
> - **Banker Bill** — wanted the farm to fail & knew the ring's worth, but on a call
>   by his car (Rita saw him).
> - **Pawnshop Pearl** — holds the real ring, but she's the witness, not the thief.
> - **Nurse Nancy** — resentful & craving pie, but by the hammock with Marty.
> - **Mailman Mike** — knew the money secrets & found the pawn ticket, but finishing
>   his route (the Hendersons saw him).
> - **Realtor Rita** — wanted the farm sold, but on a loud call by her car (Bill saw).
> - **Librarian Lou** — wanted the scandal & felt owed the ring, but reading on the
>   porch (Lily saw).
> - **Farmer Fran** — wanted the land & was in the barn, but in the MORNING; at her
>   produce stand during the salute.
> - **Ruby, Max, Lily** — the kid witnesses who cracked it wide open. Not thieves —
>   heroes.

> - **Junior Detective** — for Ruby, Max, and Lily, whose clues broke the case.

## 🎬 UNCLE PETE'S BIG MOMENT

> Now turn to the guest playing **Uncle Pete.** Their card cues them to confess,
> honest and heartfelt:
>
> *"I'm sorry. The farm was going to be gone — Dad's farm, our home. I sold his
> ring to save it, and I swear I was going to buy it back before anyone knew. I
> never meant for it to come to this. I just couldn't watch you all lose this place."*
>
> Then **Pawnshop Pearl** steps forward and returns **Grandpa's real ring** from her
> case — so it's home at last.

---

## 🇺🇸 THE FINALE — THE FORGIVENESS VOTE

> This is the heart of the whole game. The host says:
>
> **"So here's the real question, Maple family. Uncle Pete broke the rules — he took
> the pie, he kept a secret. But he did it to save the roof over all our heads.
> Tonight, YOU decide together: do we forgive Uncle Pete?"**
>
> Everyone votes out loud — thumbs up to forgive, thumbs down to convict. (It'll be
> unanimous forgiveness almost every time — that's the warm ending.) Then:
>
> **"Then it's settled. The farm is saved, the ring is home, and this family sticks
> together. Now somebody go get that pie out of the barn — we've got a fireworks
> show and a whole lot of dessert to get to. Happy 4th of July!"** 🎆🥧
