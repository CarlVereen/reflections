🔒 SECRET — read this ALONE, then keep it to yourself.

**THE TWO RULES:**
1. Never tell anyone whether your card makes you innocent or guilty.

   The ONLY thing you may invent is a REASON.

**Are you the thief?** ✅ YES — IT WAS YOU. 🤫 But you are NOT a villain.

**⚠️ THE TWO RULES STILL BIND YOU.** You may never *deny* a fact — if someone
asks whether you pawned a ring, cut your thumb, slipped away, or went to the
barn, you MUST say yes. Your entire cover is the innocent REASON, never the
denial. Stay calm, humble, and helpful — you're the last person anyone suspects.

**💔 WHY YOU DID IT (the real story):**
This spring, Maple Farm was months behind on its taxes and about to be lost —
the home Grandpa Joe built, where the whole family gathers. To save it, you
quietly took Grandpa's real gold wedding ring from the farmhouse desk, pawned
it to Pearl to make the payment, and slipped a cheap dime-store ring into the
box — meaning to buy the real one back before anyone ever knew. You told no one.
You carried it alone to protect the family — and to protect Grandma from
learning her husband's ring was gone.

**😱 WHY YOU PANICKED (the crime):**
You had NO idea Grandma baked "the ring" into her pie to give away at the
fireworks. The moment she announced it, you realized: if that pie gets cut open,
everyone sees the ring is a worthless fake — and the whole secret comes out. So
during the flag salute you slipped away, lifted the pie, and hid it in the barn
under a tarp to stop the cut. You weren't stealing dessert. You were buying time.

**🧩 THE FACTS THAT CAN CATCH YOU (all true — you can only give reasons):**
- 💍 Pawnshop Pearl saw you pawn a gold ring this spring.
- 🩹 Nurse Nancy bandaged your cut thumb this morning — and a little cousin saw
  the thief's bandaged thumb.
- 🚪 Lily saw you slip away toward the barn during the salute.
- 🚜 Max saw a covered dish carried into the barn during the salute.
- 🗄️ Grandma kept the ring in the farmhouse desk YOU use.
Each one alone is innocent. Only if a sleuth stacks ALL of them do they find you.

**🎯 WHO TO BLAME (deflect gently, never cruelly):**
- **Banker Bill** — "He wanted the farm to fail so the bank could sell it."
- **Coach Diego** — "He's the judge; he had every pie in his hands."
- **Aunt Bella** — "She's dying to finally win that ribbon."
- **Farmer Fran** — "She was in the barn all morning and wanted our land."

**⭐ AT THE REVEAL — your big, tender moment:**
When the host reveals it was you and WHY, the whole room votes on whether to
forgive you. Play it honest and heartfelt:
*"I'm sorry. The farm was going to be gone — Dad's farm, our home. I sold his
ring to save it and I was going to buy it back, I swear. I never meant for
anyone to find out. I just couldn't watch you all lose this place."*
Then Pearl returns the real ring, and the family decides your fate together.
You'll probably win Best Actor. 🎭
