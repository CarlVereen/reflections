# 👥 Character Guide (Spoiler-Free — safe for the organizer)

> This guide does **NOT** reveal the thief. Any of these characters *could* be
> the culprit as far as this page is concerned. Use it to decide who plays whom.

Give each kid the booklet that matches their character. **Players read only
their own booklet.**

---

## ⭐ The Core 6 (use these for 6 players — the mystery is fully solvable with just these)

| # | Character | In one line | Great for a kid who is... |
|---|-----------|-------------|---------------------------|
| 01 | **Grandma Rose** | The beloved baker whose blue-ribbon pie was stolen | Outgoing, dramatic, loves being the star |
| 02 | **Aunt Bella** | The fancy neighbor who always comes in 2nd at the pie contest | Confident, likes acting, can keep a straight face |
| 03 | **Grill Master Gus** | The loud, nosy king of the grill who sees everything | Funny, observant, enjoys big personalities |
| 04 | **Coach Diego** | The whistle-blowing pie-contest judge | Energetic, likes being "in charge," fair-minded |
| 05 | **Ruby** | The sneaky littlest cousin (and secret eyewitness!) | Imaginative; also great for a quieter kid |
| 06 | **Lily** | The lemonade-stand kid with a view of everything | Honest, notices things, good memory |

## ✨ Bonus 2 (add these for 7–8 players — extra fun and extra red herrings)

| # | Character | In one line | Great for a kid who is... |
|---|-----------|-------------|---------------------------|
| 07 | **Uncle Marty** | The grumpy uncle who loves Grandma's pie a little TOO much | Loves playing a "suspicious" grump; big personality |
| 08 | **Uncle Sunny** | The goofy guitar-playing song leader | Silly, performative, loves attention and laughs |

---

## 🎚️ Adjusting the group size

- **Exactly 6:** Core 6 only. ✅ Fully solvable.
- **7:** Core 6 + Uncle Marty.
- **8:** Core 6 + both Bonus characters.
- **9+:** Everyone above, plus extra kids as **Junior Detectives** (no booklet —
  they investigate, take notes, and vote). You can also print a second copy of
  a Bonus character and let two kids be "co-hosts" of that role.
- **5 or fewer:** A grown-up/older sibling plays one Core character, or one kid
  plays two. Keep all Core 6 roles filled — the case needs them.

> 🧩 **Why the Core 6 must all be present:** between them they hold the "how,"
> the "why," the eyewitness, and the alibi that cracks the case. The Bonus two
> add flavor and misdirection but aren't required. (No spoilers here — just
> trust that all six Core roles carry a piece of the puzzle.)
