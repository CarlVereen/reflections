# 👤 Who you are
You're the neighborhood coach — you run the kids' games at the cookout (sack
race, water-balloon toss) AND you're the official judge of the pie contest this
year. You're fair, friendly, and take your judging job SUPER seriously, like
it's the Olympics. You have a whistle and you're not afraid to use it. Blow it
for attention. Say "Alright, team, let's investigate — hustle!"

**Costume:** a whistle, a ball cap, a clipboard (you're judging!), sporty
clothes.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "COACH DIEGO here — I run the games AND I'm the official pie contest judge
> this year. I take that job seriously! I had all the pies lined up perfect for
> judging, and then Grandma's blue-ribbon pie just... vanished on my watch. I
> feel terrible! Let's find it, team — on the double!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You're the judge — you had ALL the pies in your hands today, including
Grandma's. Nobody had better access than you! (Defend yourself: "A judge would
NEVER! My whistle is clean and so am I!")

# 🤐 Your secret
Here's what you feel guilty about: you had already secretly decided to give
Grandma's pie first place before the contest even started, because it's clearly
the best (and, okay, she's your favorite). That's not very fair for a judge! If
anyone asks if you play favorites... get defensive. (Nothing to do with the
theft — but it makes you squirm!)

# 🔎 What you know (share these when it feels right)
- **Share freely:** "As the judge, I can tell you Grandma's pie was going to
  win by a MILE this year. So whoever took it... they didn't want it judged.
  They wanted it GONE." _(Points to motive: who benefits if Grandma's pie
  disappears?)_
- **If the contest comes up:** "Second place this year? Would've been Aunt
  Bella's cobbler — same as always. She's come in second twelve years straight."

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)
> "My alibi is rock solid: I was at the grill flipping burgers with Grill
> Master Gus during the whole flag salute. Ask Gus — we never left each other's
> side. A judge with an alibi, how about that!"

# 🎬 Act it out! (optional drama — do this during Investigation Time)
**Blow your whistle** and try to take charge: "Alright, everyone LINE UP for
questioning! Hustle!" When someone points out that YOU handled all the pies, get
very defensive and flustered ("A judge would never! NEVER!") — it makes you look
a little guilty (you're a red herring), which keeps the mystery fun.

# ❓ Questions to ask other guests
- "Who would WANT Grandma's pie out of the contest? Think, team!"
- "Aunt Bella, if Grandma's pie is gone... doesn't YOURS win?"
- "Ruby, what were you doing under that dessert table?"
