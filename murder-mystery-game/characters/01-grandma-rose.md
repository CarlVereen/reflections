# 👤 Who you are
You are the beloved grandma of the neighborhood, and your apple pie wins the
blue ribbon at the 4th of July contest EVERY single year. You're warm and
sweet... until someone messes with your pie — then watch out! You're dramatic
and a little bit of a diva about your baking. Fan yourself. Gasp a lot. Call
everyone "sweetie" and "pumpkin." You are HEARTBROKEN that your pie is gone.

**Costume:** an apron (bonus points for a flour handprint!), reading glasses,
and a sun hat.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Oh, sweeties, I'm GRANDMA ROSE, and my apple pie has won that blue ribbon
> for TWELVE years running! I set it on the dessert table to cool, turned
> around for the flag salute, and POOF — my masterpiece was GONE! Whoever took
> my pie is going to get a very stern talking-to, and NO seconds at dinner!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
Some folks think you hid your OWN pie for attention — or to avoid the contest,
so nobody would find out your little secret (see below). Act offended if
they say so!

# 🤐 Your secret
Here's the truth you'd DIE of embarrassment over: you don't actually make your
flaky crust from scratch — you buy it at the store and just add your filling!
You've let everyone believe it's 100% homemade for years. If anyone asks about
your "secret recipe," get flustered and change the subject. (This has nothing
to do with the theft — but it makes you act nervous. Perfect!)

# 🔎 What you know (share these when it feels right)
- **Share freely:** "If you ask ME, look at Coach Diego! He's the pie judge —
  he was carrying everyone's pies around all afternoon. Suspicious, if you ask
  me!" _(This makes Diego look guilty... but is that the whole story?)_
- **Only if asked about the contest:** "My biggest competition? Oh, that'd be
  Aunt Bella, bless her heart. She enters every year and always comes in
  second to me. Every. Single. Year."

# 📣 Reveal in Round 2 (announce this when the script asks)
> "Now listen here, sweeties! I set my pie on that table RIGHT before the flag
> salute, and it was sitting there pretty as a picture. So it must've been
> snatched DURING the salute — while every one of us had our hand on our heart,
> looking at the flag!"

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)
> "Me? I was standing front and center for the flag salute, singing my heart
> out — the whole crowd saw me! Why on earth would I steal my OWN blue-ribbon
> pie, pumpkin?"

# 🎬 Act it out! (optional drama — do this during Investigation Time)
Every time someone mentions your stolen pie, **swoon dramatically** into a chair
and fan yourself: "My masterpiece! Gone!" Then point at **Coach Diego** and
loudly announce, "The JUDGE had all the pies — I demand answers!" Big feelings =
big fun (and it sends detectives chasing a red herring).

# ❓ Questions to ask other guests
- "Where were you during the flag salute, sweetie?"
- "Coach Diego, you handle all the pies — care to explain yourself?"
- "Whose hands are all sticky, hmm? Let Grandma see!"

**Are you the thief?**  ❌ NO. You're innocent! (It's YOUR pie — you want it

fine to act a little flustered. That keeps the detectives guessing. You're the
