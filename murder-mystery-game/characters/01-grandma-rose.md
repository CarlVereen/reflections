# 👤 Who you are
The beloved grandma of Maple Street, whose apple pie wins the blue ribbon
EVERY year. Warm and sweet — until someone touches your pie. Then, look out!
Dramatic, fussy, calls everyone "sweetie." You are HEARTBROKEN your pie is gone.
**Costume:** apron (flour handprint = bonus!), reading glasses, sun hat.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (Round 1)
> "I'm GRANDMA ROSE, and my apple pie has won that blue ribbon for TWELVE years!
> I set it out to cool, turned around for the flag salute, and POOF — gone!
> Whoever took my pie is getting a very stern talking-to!"

# 🤐 Your secret
You don't actually make your flaky crust from scratch — you buy it at the store
and add your filling! You'd be mortified if anyone knew. Get flustered if asked
about your "secret recipe." (Nothing to do with the theft — just makes you look
nervous!)

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
Maybe you hid your OWN pie for attention — or to dodge the contest so no one
learns your store-crust secret! (Act very offended.)

# 🔎 What you know (share when it fits)
- **Freely:** "If you ask ME, look at Coach Diego — he's the judge, he had all
  the pies in his hands today!" _(A red herring... maybe.)_
- **If asked:** "My biggest rival? Aunt Bella. Enters every year, comes in
  second to me every year. Bless her heart."

# 📣 Reveal in Round 2
> "I set my pie out RIGHT before the flag salute, pretty as a picture. So it was
> snatched DURING the salute — while we all had our hands on our hearts!"

# 🧭 My alibi (Round 3)
> "I was front and center for the salute, singing away — everyone saw me! Why
> would I steal my OWN blue-ribbon pie, pumpkin?"

# 🎬 Act it out!
Every time your pie is mentioned, **swoon into a chair** and fan yourself, then
point dramatically at Coach Diego and demand answers.

# ❓ Questions to ask
- "Where were you during the flag salute, sweetie?"
- "Coach Diego, you handle all the pies — explain yourself!"
- "Whose hands are all sticky, hmm?"
