# 👤 Who you are
You're the youngest cousin at the cookout — small, quick, and always up to a
little mischief. You know all the best hiding spots in the backyard. You're shy
around grown-ups but you SEE everything from your hiding places. This is a great
part for a quieter player, because YOU are the secret eyewitness! Peek out from
behind things. Whisper your clues like they're super important (they are!).

**Costume:** comfy play clothes, maybe a juice-stain "oops," and a
mischievous grin.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Um... hi. I'm RUBY. I'm the littlest cousin. Everybody thinks I'm too small
> to notice stuff, but I notice EVERYTHING — 'cause I'm always hiding in the
> good spots. And during the flag salute, I was hiding somewhere veeeery close
> to the pie... and I saw something!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You were sneaking around the dessert table right before the pie vanished — and
everyone knows how much you love sweets. Suspicious! (Insist: "I didn't TAKE
anything... well, not the PIE, anyway.")

# 🤐 Your secret
You snuck THREE cupcakes off the dessert table before dinner and hid under the
table to eat them so you wouldn't get caught. That's actually WHY you were
under there during the flag salute — and why you saw the thief! If a grown-up
asks about missing cupcakes... look very innocent. (You're not the pie thief,
just a cupcake thief!)

# 🔎 What you know (you're the SECRET EYEWITNESS — this is huge!)
- **Hint in Round 1–2 (be a little mysterious):** "I was hiding under the
  dessert table during the flag salute. And I saw the thief's hands reach down
  and grab the pie! But I couldn't see their face..."
- **Save the big detail for your Round 3 reveal below.**

# 📣 Reveal in Round 3 (announce this when the script asks)
> "Okay, here's the big thing I saw! From under the table, I saw the thief's
> hands grab Grandma's pie — and their hands were all STICKY and covered in
> gooey apple pie filling! So... EVERYBODY show me your hands! The thief's
> hands would've been super messy!"

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)
> "My alibi? I was under the dessert table the whole time, eating you-know-what.
> So I couldn't have grabbed the pie — my hands were full of cupcake!"

# 🎬 Act it out! (optional drama — do this during Investigation Time)
Sneak around behind people and **duck out of sight** whenever a grown-up
character looks your way (you're the sneaky cousin!). Let someone "catch" you
with cupcake crumbs on your face. Then, at your big moment, jump up and shout,
"WAIT — I saw something!" and make everyone show their hands.

# ❓ Questions to ask other guests
- "Let me see your hands! Are they sticky? Are they?!"
- "Were you anywhere near the dessert table during the salute?"
- "Aunt Bella, why do you keep hiding your hands?"
