# 👤 Who you are
You run the lemonade stand at the cookout — 25 cents a cup! You're cheerful,
hardworking, and a little bit of a businessperson (you keep track of every
penny). Your stand is set up right by the back kitchen door, so you have a
perfect view of who goes in and out of the house. You're the LOOKOUT of this
mystery! Ring an imaginary bell. Say "Lemonade! Ice cold! Get your lemonade!"

**Costume:** an apron and a paper sign that says "LEMONADE 25¢." Carry a cup
or pitcher.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Step right up — I'm LILY, and I run the best lemonade stand on Maple Street!
> My stand is right next to the kitchen door, so I saw EVERYBODY coming and
> going all day. And I'll tell you what — I noticed something reeeally strange
> about who did NOT go into that kitchen. Lemonade? Only a quarter!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
Some folks say you wanted the pie contest cancelled so everyone would get bored
and buy more lemonade instead. More chaos = more sales! (Protest: "I sell
lemonade, not stolen pie! That's bad for business!")

# 🤐 Your secret
Business was slow, so you secretly watered down the lemonade to make it stretch
further — so you're basically selling flavored water for a quarter. If anyone
complains the lemonade tastes weak... change the subject fast! (Nothing to do
with the theft, but it makes you sweat a little.)

# 🔎 What you know (you're a KEY witness — the alibi-breaker!)
- **Hint early:** "My stand is right by the kitchen door, and I watched it all
  day. Funny thing — during the flag salute, NObody went into that kitchen. Not

- **Save the big line for your Round 3 reveal below.**

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)

> everybody could see me. I even sold three cups during the flag salute!"

and **butt into private conversations** to share what you overheard from your
stand ("I heard that! Let me tell you what ELSE I saw..."). You see and hear
everything by that kitchen door, so act like the eyes and ears of the party.
