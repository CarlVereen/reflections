# 👤 Who you are
You grow the apples the whole town uses — including the ones in Grandma's
famous pie. You're proud, hardworking, and a little grumpy that nobody ever
credits YOUR apples for Grandma's blue ribbons. Say "Those are MY apples, you
know."
**Costume:** overalls, a straw hat, a bandana, maybe a basket of apples.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (Round 1)
> "FARMER FRAN, and I grow the best apples in three counties! Grandma's pie
> wins every year with MY apples, and does she ever thank me? Not once! But
> stealing her pie wouldn't get me any credit either, would it?"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You've got a real grudge — Grandma takes all the glory for pies made from your
apples. Maybe you finally snapped! (Huff: "I grow apples, I don't STEAL pies!")

# 🤐 Your secret
Your orchard had a bad year, so the "prize apples" you bragged about bringing
are actually from the grocery store. Don't let the farmers' pride crowd find
out!

# 🔎 What you know
- **If asked:** "I was over at my produce stand the whole salute, selling
  apples and corn. Lots of folks bought from me — ask around!"
- **Helpful:** "My stand sits right by the grill — I'll vouch that Grill Master
  Gus and Coach Diego never left it during the salute, flipping burgers the whole
  time, the pair of them."
- **Flavor:** "That pie's filling? Cinnamon and MY apples. You could smell it
  from a mile off." _(Reminds everyone the pie leaves an apple-cinnamon scent
  and trail.)_

# 🧭 My alibi (Round 3)
> "My produce stand, the whole time. I sold a dozen ears of corn during the
> salute alone. My customers will vouch for me!"

# 🎬 Act it out!
Grumble loudly that nobody credits your apples. Try to **sell produce** to the
sleuths mid-investigation: "Apple while you sleuth? Two for a dollar!"

# 🕵️ Who YOU suspect (say it loud — accuse them!)
You blame **BAKER BENNY**. Your reason: "That fancy baker uses a boxed MIX and
STILL loses to Grandma every year. A man that proud would absolutely cheat to
win."

# ❓ Questions to ask
- "Anybody smell apple-cinnamon? That's the pie's trail!"
- "Where'd that sticky trail lead, anyhow?"
- "Who's NEVER given me credit for my apples? Suspicious, if you ask me."
