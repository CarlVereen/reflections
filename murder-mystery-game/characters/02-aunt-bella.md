# 👤 Who you are
You're the fancy, competitive neighbor who enters the pie contest EVERY year...
and comes in second to Grandma Rose EVERY year. You're all smiles and sugar on
the outside, but inside you are SO tired of losing. You act sweet, helpful, and
totally innocent. Wave to everyone. Compliment their outfits. Seem like the
last person who'd ever cause trouble. 😇

**Costume:** your "fanciest" cookout outfit, big sunglasses, and carry your own
pie carrier.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Oh, hello, everyone! I'm AUNT BELLA. I bake a pie for the contest every year
> — a LOVELY peach cobbler, if I do say so myself. I always come in second to
> dear Grandma Rose, of course. But steal her pie? Me?! I would NEVER. Now,
> let's all work together to find the real culprit, shall we?"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You've lost to Grandma twelve years in a row — everyone knows how badly you
want that blue ribbon. With her pie gone... yours would finally win. Hmm!
(If someone points this out, laugh it off: "Oh, that's a silly thing to say!")

# 🔎 What you know (use these to seem helpful and point elsewhere)
- **Say early and often:** "If you ask me, we should look at Coach Diego — he's
  the judge, he had ALL the pies in his hands today! Or maybe little Ruby, she
  was sneaking around the dessert table..." _(Steer attention anywhere but you.)_
- **Be helpful:** offer to "organize the search" and keep everyone busy.

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3 — but be careful!)
> "During the flag salute? Oh, I popped inside to the kitchen to refill the
> lemonade pitcher — such a good helper, me! I didn't see a thing out here."

_(⚠️ This is your cover story. It is NOT true — and someone may be able to prove
you were never in that kitchen. Stay calm if they do! See your secret below.)_

# 🎬 Act it out! (optional drama — do this during Investigation Time)
Early on, have a loud but SWEET "friendly" argument with Grandma Rose about who
REALLY deserves the blue ribbon ("My cobbler is every bit as good, dear!") —
then instantly switch to being super nice, like you didn't mean it. And any time
someone looks at your hands, quickly hide them behind your back or wipe them on a
napkin. (It builds your motive AND your bluff!)

# ❓ Questions to ask other guests
- "Coach Diego, weren't YOU holding all the pies today?"
- "Did anyone actually SEE the thief? No? Well then!"
- "Shouldn't we all just relax and let ME handle the investigating?"

🔒 SECRET — read this part ALONE. This is the biggest secret in the game.
Do NOT let anyone see this page!

**Are you the thief?**  ✅ YES. IT WAS YOU! 🤫

You stole Grandma Rose's blue-ribbon apple pie. Here's the truth (keep it
secret and BLUFF!):
- **Why you did it:** You are SICK of coming in second place. With Grandma's
  pie gone, YOUR pie finally wins the blue ribbon this year!
- **How you did it:** You brought your own big **pie carrier** to the cookout as
  a decoy. During the flag salute, when everyone faced the flag with their
  hands on their hearts, you slipped to the dessert table, used **oven mitts**
  to grab the warm pie, hid it in your pie carrier, and stashed it under your
  **lawn chair**.
- **Your mistakes (this is how you might get caught!):**
  1. The warm pie dripped **apple-cinnamon filling** — there's a sticky trail
     leading toward your lawn chair, and a fork with fresh pie right by your
     seat! 🍴
  2. You got apple filling **all over your hands** while grabbing it. 👐
  3. You told everyone you were "in the kitchen," but the lemonade-stand kid
     (Lily) was right by the kitchen door the whole time and never saw you.
  4. You dropped a note about being "tired of second place."

**How to bluff (this is the fun part — you're the star!):**
- Act sweet, calm, and super helpful.
- If someone wants to check hands, wipe yours on a napkin fast, or sit on them,
  or say "we don't have time for that, sweetie!"
- Keep pointing at **Coach Diego** (the judge) or **anyone** else.
- If accused, deny it politely: "Oh, what a thing to say! I'm just here for the
  cookout. You have no proof!"

**⭐ When the SOLUTION is read at the end:** it's your big moment! Stand up and
give a dramatic CONFESSION. Something like: *"FINE! It was me! Twelve years of
second place — I just wanted ONE blue ribbon! You clever, clever detectives —
you caught me!"* Take a bow. You'll probably win Best Actor. 🎭
