# 👤 Who you are
The fancy, competitive neighbor who enters the pie contest EVERY year and comes
in second to Grandma Rose EVERY year. All smiles and sugar outside — but tired
of losing inside. Act sweet, helpful, totally innocent.
**Costume:** your "fanciest" cookout outfit, big sunglasses, carry a pie carrier.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (Round 1)
> "Oh, hello! I'm AUNT BELLA. I bake a lovely peach cobbler for the contest
> every year — always second to dear Grandma Rose, of course. But steal her pie?
> I would NEVER! Let's find the real culprit, shall we?"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You've lost to Grandma twelve years running — everyone knows how badly you want
that ribbon. With her pie gone... yours would finally win. (Laugh it off!)

# 🔎 What you know (use it to point elsewhere)
- **Often:** "If you ask me, look at Coach Diego — he had ALL the pies! Or maybe
  little Ruby, sneaking around the dessert table..."
- Offer to "organize the search" and keep everyone busy.

# 🧭 My alibi (Round 3 — be careful!)
> "During the salute? I popped into the kitchen to refill the lemonade — such a
> good helper! Didn't see a thing."

_(⚠️ Cover story. NOT true — someone may prove you were never in that kitchen.
Stay calm! See below.)_

# 🎬 Act it out!
Early on, have a loud but SWEET "friendly" argument with Grandma about who
deserves the ribbon, then instantly switch to being super nice. Any time someone
eyes your hands, hide them behind your back.

# ❓ Questions to ask
- "Coach Diego, weren't YOU holding all the pies?"
- "Did anyone actually SEE the thief? No? Well then!"
- "Let's all relax and let ME handle the investigating."

------------------------------------------------------------
🔒 SECRET — read ALONE. Biggest secret in the game. Don't let anyone see this!

**Are you the thief?** ✅ YES. IT WAS YOU! 🤫
- **Why:** You're sick of second place. With Grandma's pie gone, YOURS wins!
- **How:** You brought your own **pie carrier** as a decoy and borrowed Grill
  Master Gus's **oven mitts**. During the flag salute, you slipped to the
  dessert table, grabbed the warm pie, hid it in your carrier, and stashed it
  under your **lawn chair**.
- **Your mistakes (how you get caught):**
  1. 🍎 Sticky apple-cinnamon drips lead to your lawn chair — with a fork of
     fresh pie beside it!
  2. 👐 You got apple filling all over your hands.
  3. 🍋 You said you were "in the kitchen," but the lemonade kid (Lily) was by
     the kitchen door the whole time and never saw you.
  4. 📝 You dropped a note about being "tired of second place."
- **Bluff:** Act sweet and helpful. Hide/wipe your hands. Keep pointing at Coach
  Diego or anyone else. If accused: "What a thing to say! You have no proof!"

**⭐ At the reveal:** big dramatic CONFESSION — *"FINE! Twelve years of second
place — I just wanted ONE blue ribbon!"* Take a bow. Best Actor, probably. 🎭
