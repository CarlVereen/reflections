# 👤 Who you are
You're the grumpy uncle who just wants to nap in the hammock and eat pie in
peace. You complain about EVERYTHING — the heat, the bugs, the noisy kids. But
deep down you're a big softie, and you LOVE Grandma Rose's apple pie more than
anything in the world. Grumble a lot. Cross your arms. Say "Back in MY day..."
and "Can't a fella nap around here?"

**Costume:** sunglasses, flip-flops, a big grumpy frown, and a napkin tucked
in your collar (ready for pie).

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Ugh, fine, I'll introduce myself. UNCLE MARTY. I came here to nap in that
> hammock and eat a big slice of Grandma's pie. Now the pie's GONE and everyone's
> hollering. Great. Just great. This is the worst 4th of July ever. Somebody
> find that pie so I can EAT it!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
Everyone knows you love Grandma's pie more than any human alive — you once ate
an ENTIRE pie by yourself at a picnic. If anyone would swipe it just to eat
it... it's you! (Grumble: "If I took it, I'd have EATEN it already! Do you SEE
any pie? No!")

# 🤐 Your secret
You secretly enter the pie contest every single year under a fake name ("Marv
the Marvelous") and you always lose — and it makes you SO grumpy. You'd be
mortified if anyone found out that's you. (Nothing to do with the theft, but
it's a fun thing to hide!)

# 🔎 What you know (a helpful clue from the hammock!)
- **Share when it comes up:** "During the flag salute, I was half-asleep in my
  hammock. But I DID crack one eye open and see somebody hurry past me carrying
  a big covered dish — headed straight toward the lawn chairs. Didn't see who,
  though. I was busy napping!" _(Points the trail toward the lawn chairs!)_

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)
> "My alibi? I was flat on my back in that hammock the WHOLE time — ask anybody,
> I hadn't moved in an hour. My knee's too creaky to go sprinting after pies,
> and that thief moved FAST. Wasn't me. Now, is there any pie left or not?"

# 🎬 Act it out! (optional drama — do this during Investigation Time)
**Pretend to snooze** in a chair (your "hammock"), then "wake up" with a loud
snort every time something exciting happens: "Wha—? Did somebody find my pie?!"
Grumpily accuse random people ("It was probably YOU!") and demand pie
constantly. Big grump energy makes everyone laugh.

# ❓ Questions to ask other guests
- "Who was rushing toward the lawn chairs during the salute, huh?"
- "Whatcha hiding under YOUR lawn chair, then?"
- "Who's got pie on their breath? Come here, let me smell ya!"
