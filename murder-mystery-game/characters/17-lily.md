# 👤 Who you are
You run the lemonade stand — 25 cents a cup! Cheerful, hardworking, and a little
bit of a businessperson (you count every penny). Your stand sits right by the
back kitchen door, so you see who goes in and out of the house. You're the
LOOKOUT! Ring an imaginary bell: "Lemonade! Ice cold!"
**Costume:** an apron and a "LEMONADE 25¢" sign. Carry a cup or pitcher.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (Round 1)
> "Step right up — I'm LILY, best lemonade stand on Maple Street! My stand's
> right by the kitchen door, so I saw EVERYBODY come and go. And I noticed
> something reeeally strange about who did NOT go in that kitchen!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
Maybe you wanted the contest cancelled so bored guests would buy more lemonade!
More chaos, more sales. (Protest: "I sell lemonade, not stolen pie!")

# 🤐 Your secret
Business was slow, so you watered down the lemonade to stretch it — you're
basically selling flavored water. Change the subject if anyone says it tastes
weak!

# 🔎 What you know (the ALIBI-BREAKER!)
- **Hint early:** "My stand's right by the kitchen door. Funny thing — during
  the salute, NObody went into that kitchen. Not one person."
- **Save the big line for Round 3.**

# 📣 Reveal in Round 3
> "Here's what I KNOW: I was at my stand by the kitchen door the WHOLE party. And
> during the flag salute, NOBODY went into that kitchen — nobody! So if someone
> says they were 'inside getting lemonade' during the salute... that's a big
> fat FIB!"

# 🧭 My alibi (Round 3)
> "I was at my lemonade stand the whole time, where everyone could see me — I
> even sold three cups during the salute!"

# 🎬 Act it out!
Keep "working" your stand — "Ice-cold lemonade, 25 cents!" — and **butt into
private conversations** to share what you overheard.

# ❓ Questions to ask
- "Where exactly were you during the flag salute?"
- "Did anyone say they went INSIDE? Because I'd know!"
- "Aunt Bella — you said the kitchen, but I never saw you go in!"
