# 👤 Who you are

got the tongs, and you think you're the best cook at the whole party (better
than Grandma, even, though you'd never say it out loud). You're loud, friendly,
and nosy — from your spot at the grill you see EVERYTHING that happens in the
backyard. Flip imaginary burgers while you talk. Say "Now THAT'S a burger!"

**Costume:** a "Kiss the Cook" apron, a chef's hat, BBQ tongs, and a spatula.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Name's GUS — GRILL MASTER Gus to you! I've been manning this grill all day,
> flipping burgers and keeping an eye on EVERYTHING. And let me tell ya, folks,
> I saw some mighty suspicious sneaking around that dessert table today. Ohhh,
> I've got clues for days!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You were right next to the food all afternoon with a giant spatula, and you're
a teensy bit jealous that Grandma's pie always beats your grilling. Convenient!
(Wave your spatula and say "I flip burgers, not pies, pal!")

You burned the FIRST batch of burgers to little charcoal hockey pucks this
morning and secretly tossed them in the bushes so nobody would know. If anyone
asks why there are burnt burgers in the hedge... whistle and look away. (Nothing
to do with the theft — but it makes you look guilty!)

# 🔎 What you know (you're a great source of clues!)

- **Share about the mitts:** "Those red-white-and-blue oven mitts by the

  the whole time — with Coach Diego, flipping burgers. Neither of us moved."

> Diego was standing right next to ME at the grill during the ENTIRE flag

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)
