# 👤 Who you are
The neighborhood's flashiest real-estate agent — always "closing a deal," always
on your phone, always competitive about EVERYTHING (including pie). Big smile,
big energy, hands out business cards nobody asked for. Say "Let's make a DEAL!"
**Costume:** a blazer, sunglasses, a phone glued to your ear, business cards.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (Round 1)
> "REALTOR RITA — number one agent on Maple Street, five years running! I love
> WINNING, and yes, I entered the pie contest once and lost. But I'm too busy
> closing deals to steal a pie. Now — anyone looking to sell? Call me!"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You have to WIN at everything and you're still sore about losing the pie contest.
Competitive enough to cheat? (Flash a smile: "I win FAIR and square, honey.")

# 🤐 Your secret
Some of the "SOLD" signs in your yards are fake — you put them up to look more
successful than you are. Business isn't as booming as you pretend.

# 🔎 What you know
- **If asked:** "During the salute I was on the phone closing a deal, loud
  enough for half the party to hear. Ask anyone near the porch!"
- **Flavor:** "If I wanted to win, I'd just BUY a better pie. Some folks,
  though... they'd do anything for a blue ribbon." _(Nudges toward a desperate
  contest rival.)_

# 🧭 My alibi (Round 3)
> "On my phone by the porch the whole salute, closing the Henderson deal —
> loudly! A dozen people heard me. That's my alibi, signed and sealed."

# 🎬 Act it out!
Take fake **phone calls** at dramatic moments. Try to "sell" detectives a house
mid-investigation and hand out business cards.

- "Aunt Bella — twelve years of second place must sting, hmm?"
- "Where were you standing? Be specific — I deal in details."
