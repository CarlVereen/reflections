# 👤 Who you are
You're the fun uncle who brings the guitar to every party and leads everyone in
songs. Today you led the whole crowd in the flag salute and the national anthem
— which means everybody was looking at YOU (and the flag) when the pie
disappeared! You're cheerful, goofy, and love being the center of attention.
Strum an imaginary guitar. Break into song at random. Say "Everybody, sing
along!"

**Costume:** a bright Hawaiian shirt, a toy guitar or ukulele, and a huge
smile.

# 🗣️ Introduce yourself out loud (read this in Round 1)
> "Helloooo, Maple Street! I'm UNCLE SUNNY, and I brought my guitar to lead the
> flag salute today! Everybody was singing along, hands on hearts, eyes on the
> flag... which means NOBODY was watching that pie table. Uh-oh. I think I
> accidentally gave the thief the perfect cover! Whoops-a-daisy. 🎸"

# 🎯 Why people might suspect YOU
You made ALL that noise and got everyone to look away from the food — the
perfect distraction! Maybe you planned it that way? (Laugh: "I lead songs, not
heists! Though it WAS a great distraction, if I do say so myself.")

# 🤐 Your secret
Last week you accidentally broke Grandma Rose's favorite serving platter and
hid the broken pieces in the garage, hoping she wouldn't notice. If Grandma
asks about her platter... start playing a loud song to drown out the question!
(Nothing to do with the theft — just an unrelated "oops.")

# 🔎 What you know (a helpful clue about the timing!)
- **Share freely:** "Here's the thing — while I was leading the anthem, EVERY
  single person had their eyes on me and the flag. That's EXACTLY when the pie
  got snatched. So the thief is someone who slipped away during my song while
  we were all distracted!" _(Confirms the crime happened during the salute.)_
- **If asked:** "Could I have done it? Nope — I was up front strumming away in
  front of the whole crowd the entire time. Best alibi at the party!"

# 🧭 My alibi (read this in Round 3)
> "My alibi is the BEST one here — I was standing in front of everybody leading
> the anthem the whole time! Twenty people watched me play. You can't sneak off
> to steal a pie when you're the one on stage, folks!"

# 🎬 Act it out! (optional drama — do this during Investigation Time)
Whenever someone asks about Grandma's broken platter (your secret!), **burst
into a loud song** and strum your guitar to drown them out: "🎶 Ohhh say can you
seeee... 🎶". Randomly lead little sing-alongs to "distract" the detectives.
You're the goofy, noisy heart of the party!

# ❓ Questions to ask other guests
- "So — who DIDN'T I see in the crowd during my song, hmm?"
- "Who slipped away while everyone was singing along?"
- "Where'd you sneak off to during the anthem?"
