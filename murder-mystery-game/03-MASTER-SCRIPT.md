# 📣 THE PIE THAT WASN'T — Master Script (Host Reads Aloud)

> **This script runs the whole party.** You are the **HOST / NARRATOR** — you don't
> play a character, you emcee. Read the **bold quotes** out loud in a big, fun
> voice. Everything in *(italics)* is a stage direction just for you.
>
> **Timing:** the whole game runs about **75–90 minutes.** Three mingle rounds of
> ~15 minutes each, with a public clue between them, then voting and the reveal.
>
> Keep the **SOLUTION** (`99-SOLUTION-DO-NOT-OPEN.md`) and the three **clue
> envelopes** (`clues/`) nearby. Don't peek at the solution in front of guests.

---

## ⏱️ BEFORE YOU START (checklist)
- [ ] Every guest has their **character card** and **name tag** (see `characters/`).
- [ ] Everyone has read their own card **alone** and kept it secret.
- [ ] Each guest has a **ballot** and a pencil (see `clues/name-tags-and-ballots.md`).
- [ ] The **prop pie** is hidden in (or near) the "barn" — your hiding spot.
- [ ] Three **clue envelopes** are set aside, labeled 1, 2, 3.
- [ ] You've read this script and the solution once, on your own.

---

## 🎬 OPENING — gather everyone (read aloud)

> **"Happy Fourth of July, and welcome to the Maple Farm cookout and pie contest!
> For years, Grandma Rose's apple pie has taken the blue ribbon — but this year
> she told us something extra special. Grandma?"**
>
> *(Cue the guest playing Grandma Rose. She announces, or you say it for her:)*
>
> **"Baked right inside this year's pie is Grandpa Joe's gold wedding ring — and at
> the fireworks tonight, she's giving it to one lucky grandchild as our family
> heirloom. Folks, let's all face the flag for the salute before we judge the pies!"**
>
> *(Pause dramatically. Then, alarmed:)*
>
> **"...Wait. Where's the pie? GRANDMA'S PIE IS GONE! It was right here on the
> dessert table during the salute — and now it's VANISHED, ring and all!**
>
> **Somebody at this cookout took it. Every single one of you is a suspect — and
> every single one of you is also a detective. Here are the rules..."**

---

## 📜 THE RULES (read aloud — this is the heart of the game)

> **"Three simple rules, everybody:**
>
> **1. Your card is TRUE. Everything on your card really happened — who you are, why
> you're here, what you saw, and your motives. When someone asks you a straight
> question about a fact on your card, you MUST tell the truth. No lying about facts!**
>
> **2. But you CAN spin a REASON. The thief's whole trick is that every true fact
> has an innocent excuse. So you can explain WHY you did something — that's where
> the bluffing and fun live. The fact is locked; the reason is yours to sell.**
>
> **3. Never say whether you're innocent or guilty. Everybody act a little suspicious!
> Work the room, ask questions, compare stories, and figure out who took the pie.**
>
> **At the end, EVERYONE votes on a ballot — grownups and kids alike. Nail the thief
> and you're a Master Sleuth. Ready? Start mingling!"**

*(Tip for the host: circulate and nudge shy players. Point kids toward Ruby, Max,
and Lily, who hold the biggest clues. If anyone gets stuck, remind them: "Ask what
they were doing during the salute!")*

---

## 🕵️ ROUND 1 — "Where was everyone?" (~15 min)

*(Let guests mingle freely. Everyone's goal this round: find out where each person
was during the flag salute, and who slipped away. After ~15 minutes, ring a bell /
clink a glass and read Clue Envelope 1.)*

> **"Sleuths, gather up! Our first clue just came in..."**
>
> *(Open and read **`clues/clue-envelope-1.md`** aloud. Then:)*
>
> **"Keep that in mind — and keep digging. Round two!"**

---

## 🕵️ ROUND 2 — "It was never about the pie" (~15 min)

*(Guests mingle again, now chasing motives and money troubles. After ~15 minutes,
ring the bell and read Clue Envelope 2.)*

> **"Everyone in! Clue number two, and this one's a twist..."**
>
> *(Open and read **`clues/clue-envelope-2.md`** aloud. Then:)*
>
> **"Chew on THAT. What if the thief never wanted the pie at all? Round three —
> find out what those two little cousins saw!"**

---

## 🕵️ ROUND 3 — "Triangulate the thief" (~15 min)

*(Final mingle. Players should now be comparing the kids' clues — the bandaged
thumb, the barn, who slipped away. After ~15 minutes, ring the bell and read Clue
Envelope 3.)*

> **"Last clue, everybody — this one ties it all together..."**
>
> *(Open and read **`clues/clue-envelope-3.md`** aloud. Then:)*
>
> **"You've got everything you need. One person had the motive, the pawned ring, the
> bandaged thumb, slipped away during the salute, AND access to Grandpa's ring. Talk
> it out — you've got five minutes before we vote!"**

---

## 🗳️ THE VOTE (read aloud)

> **"Time to vote! On your ballot, write down TWO things:**
> **1. WHO took the pie?**
> **2. WHY did they do it? (What's the real reason?)**
>
> **No teaming up — this is YOUR guess. Fold it up and pass it to me."**

*(Collect all ballots. Set them where you can read them at the reveal. Build
suspense:)*

> **"The votes are in. Before I reveal the thief... let me tell you the story of
> what REALLY happened at Maple Farm this afternoon."**

---

## 📣 THE REVEAL

*(Now open **`99-SOLUTION-DO-NOT-OPEN.md`** and read it aloud, section by section:
the reveal of Uncle Pete → why he did it → how the clues fit → score the ballots →
Pete's confession → the forgiveness vote → cut open the prop pie. Take your time and
milk the twist — the "why" is the whole payoff.)*

---

## 🏅 AWARDS & WRAP-UP

*(After the forgiveness vote, hand out the certificates from
`clues/name-tags-and-ballots.md`:)*

> **"Let's hear it for our MASTER SLEUTHS — everyone who named Uncle Pete!"**
> *(applause)*
> **"Our SHARPEST SLEUTH, who also nailed the real reason!"**
> **"Our JUNIOR DETECTIVES — Ruby, Max, and Lily, who cracked this wide open!"**
> **"And BEST ACTOR — Uncle Pete himself, for breaking all our hearts!"**
>
> **"The farm is saved, the ring is home, and this family sticks together. Now —
> fireworks, and PIE! Happy Fourth of July, Maple family!"** 🎆🥧🇺🇸

---

### 🆘 HOST QUICK-FIXES
- **Someone solved it too fast?** Ask them to keep it secret and help you drop hints
  to others — everyone still votes at the end.
- **Nobody's getting it?** Steer people to Pearl ("ask her who pawned a ring"), then
  to Lily and Max ("who slipped away? where did the pie go?").
- **A kid is overwhelmed?** Their one big clue is enough — have them just repeat what
  they saw to anyone who asks. They'll feel like a hero at the reveal.
- **Fewer than 18 guests?** See `characters/00-CHARACTER-GUIDE.md` for which roles
  are essential vs. droppable.
