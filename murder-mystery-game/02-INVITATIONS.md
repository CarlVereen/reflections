# 💌 Invitations & Costume Ideas

Print, cut out, and hand these to guests — or copy the text into a text/email.
Fill in the blanks before sending.

---

## ✂️ Printable Invitation (cut along the dashes)

```
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

     🎆  YOU'RE INVITED TO A 4TH OF JULY MYSTERY!  🎆
              THE PIE THAT WASN'T
            A Mystery at Maple Farm

  Come to the Maple Farm cookout for burgers, games, and
  fireworks... but there's TROUBLE! Grandma Rose baked
  Grandpa's gold wedding ring right into her blue-ribbon
  apple pie — and during the flag salute, the whole pie
  VANISHED. One of us took it. We need EVERYONE to help
  figure out who... and why. 🥧🕵️

     🗓️  When:  ____________________________________

     📍  Where: ____________________________________

     ⏰  Time:  ____________  to  ____________

  ★ You'll play a real cookout character with secrets and clues!
  ★ Wear RED, WHITE & BLUE if you can (see the back for your role).
  ★ Everyone investigates, and everyone votes on whodunit at the end!

     RSVP to: __________________  by: ______________

        Your character will be waiting when you arrive...

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
```

> **Tip:** On each guest's invite, write which character they'll be playing
> (from the Character Guide) so they can plan a costume. Keep it a *little*
> mysterious — tell them their name and one fun detail, not their secrets!

## 🎭 Costume ideas (all easy, all optional)

**Everyone can wear:** anything red, white, and blue — a flag T-shirt, star
sunglasses, a stars-and-stripes bandana, a patriotic hat, face paint, or
sparkly star stickers. 🇺🇸

**By character** (send each guest just their own):
- **Grandma Rose** — an apron (a flour handprint = bonus points!), reading glasses,
  a sun hat. You're the star baker!
- **Uncle Pete** — work jeans, a plaid shirt, a green farm cap, and a bandage on
  your thumb. You're the quiet farm-keeper.
- **Aunt Sunny** — a bright sundress, a flag pin, a big sun hat. You're all sunshine.
- **Aunt Bella** — your "fanciest" cookout outfit, big sunglasses, and carry a pie
  carrier. You came to WIN the contest.
- **Uncle Marty** — sunglasses, suspenders, a "World's Grumpiest Uncle" scowl, and a
  big appetite for pie.
- **Cousin Charlie** — flashy-but-worn clothes, a phone, and a bandage on your hand.
  You're always chasing the next big deal.
- **Coach Diego** — a whistle, a ball cap, a clipboard (you're the pie judge!),
  sporty clothes.
- **Grill Master Gus** — a "Kiss the Cook" apron, a chef's hat, and BBQ tongs. You
  run the grill!
- **Banker Bill** — a short-sleeve dress shirt, a tie, and a briefcase you didn't
  need to bring.
- **Pawnshop Pearl** — lots of costume jewelry and a jeweler's loupe on a chain.
- **Nurse Nancy** — scrubs, a first-aid pouch, sensible shoes.
- **Mailman Mike** — a postal-blue shirt, a mailbag, sunglasses.
- **Realtor Rita** — a blazer, sunglasses, a phone glued to your ear, business cards.
- **Librarian Lou** — a cardigan, glasses, a book under your arm.
- **Farmer Fran** — overalls, a straw hat, work gloves (a little dirt = bonus!).
- **Ruby** — comfy play clothes, a juice-stain "oops," and a mischievous grin.
- **Max** — shorts, a ball cap, and a bandaged knee. You're the adventurer!
- **Lily** — an apron, a sun visor, and a paper sign that says "LEMONADE 50¢."
