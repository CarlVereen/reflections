# 💌 Invitations & Costume Ideas

Print, cut out, and hand these to guests — or copy the text into a text/email.
Fill in the blanks before sending.

---

## ✂️ Printable Invitation (cut along the dashes)

```
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

     🎆  YOU'RE INVITED TO A 4TH OF JULY MYSTERY!  🎆
        The Case of the Missing Blue-Ribbon Pie

  Come to the Maple Street Cookout for burgers, games, and
  fireworks... but there's TROUBLE! Grandma Rose's famous
  blue-ribbon apple pie has been STOLEN right off the dessert
  table — and one of the guests is the thief! We need YOU to
  help solve the mystery. 🥧🕵️

     🗓️  When:  ____________________________________

     📍  Where: ____________________________________

     ⏰  Time:  ____________  to  ____________

  ★ You'll play a real cookout character with secrets and clues!
  ★ Wear RED, WHITE & BLUE if you can (see the back for your role).
  ★ There'll be snacks, games, and a mystery to crack. See you there!

     RSVP to: __________________  by: ______________

        Your character will be waiting when you arrive...

- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
```

> **Tip:** On each guest's invite, write which character they'll be playing
> (from the Character Guide) so they can plan a costume. Keep it a *little*
> mysterious — tell them their name and one fun detail, not their secrets!

## 🎭 Costume ideas (all easy, all optional)

**Everyone can wear:** anything red, white, and blue — a flag T-shirt, star
sunglasses, a stars-and-stripes bandana, a patriotic hat, face paint, or
sparkly star stickers. 🇺🇸

**By character** (send each guest just their own):
- **Grandma Rose** — an apron (a flour handprint = bonus points!), reading
  glasses, a sun hat. You're the star baker!
- **Aunt Bella** — your "fanciest" cookout outfit, big sunglasses, and carry
  your own pie carrier. You came to WIN.
- **Grill Master Gus** — a "Kiss the Cook" apron, a chef's hat, BBQ tongs, and
  a spatula. You run the grill!
- **Coach Diego** — a whistle, a ball cap, a clipboard (you're the pie judge
  today!), sporty clothes.
- **Ruby** — comfy play clothes, maybe a juice-stain "oops" and a mischievous
  grin. You're the sneaky little kid.
- **Lily** — an apron and a paper sign that says "LEMONADE 25¢." Carry a cup or
  pitcher. You run the lemonade stand!
- **Uncle Marty** — sunglasses, flip-flops, a "World's Grumpiest Uncle" vibe,
  and a big appetite for pie.
- **Uncle Sunny** — a bright Hawaiian shirt, a toy guitar or ukulele, and a
  huge smile. You lead the songs!
