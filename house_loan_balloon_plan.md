## The exits (a balloon almost never means paying $130k in cash)

1. **Refinance into a normal mortgage** — replace the balloon with a
   15/30-yr loan. Most common path. Requires *qualifying* → income + credit.
   This is what the $7k/month goal and the GEICO job are really for.
2. **Extend / modify with the current lender** — call whoever holds the note
   now, *before* the due date. Lenders usually prefer extending over
   foreclosing. Buys time, often with less qualifying. Best emergency move.
3. **Sell the house** — if it's worth meaningfully more than $130k, selling
   clears the balloon and hands you the equity. Needs no job, no new loan.
   **Depends entirely on the home's current value vs. $130k.**
4. **HELOC / home-equity** — depends on equity; usually still needs income.

### Do NOT do this
- Cover a $130k mortgage balloon with an **unsecured personal loan**
  (Upstart etc.). Wrong tool, high rate, and new debt hurts refi qualifying.

## Second number that matters: home value vs. $130k

- Worth ~$250k → ~$120k equity. "Sell" is a safety net; you can't lose the
  money. Refi is easy. Most of the panic is unfounded.
- Worth ~$135k → thin equity. Refi/extend is the real game.
- Underwater → urgent; move fast on lender modification.

Get a rough value: Zillow/Redfin estimate now, a realtor's opinion later.
