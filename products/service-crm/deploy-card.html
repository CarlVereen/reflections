Turn on your app: 6 taps

Do this once, on a computer, to get your app link. After that you just open the link (add it to your phone's home screen). It all runs in your own Google account.

1
Open the code editor
In your sheet's top menu: Extensions → Apps Script. It opens in a new tab.
2
Start a deployment
Top-right blue button: Deploy → New deployment.
3
Choose "Web app"
Click the ⚙️ gear (Select type) → pick Web app.
4
Set who can use it
Execute as: Me  ·  Who has access: Only myself. (Keeps it private to you.)
5
Deploy & allow
Deploy → Authorize access → Allow. The "hasn't verified / unsafe" screen is normal. It's your own copy.
6
Copy your app link
Copy the Web app URL. Open it. That's your app. On your phone: browser menu → Add to Home Screen.
✅ Done. That link is your CRM. Reload it any time.
⏰ Set your timezone once so scheduled jobs hit your calendar at the right hour: Apps Script ▸ Project Settings (⚙️) ▸ Time zone → pick yours.
Can't find the link again? In your sheet: ⚡ CRM ▸ 📲 Open the app (get link) shows it anytime.
On a computer and see "unable to open the file"? You're signed into more than one Google account. Open the app link in an Incognito window signed in with just the account that owns the sheet (or make that account your default).
