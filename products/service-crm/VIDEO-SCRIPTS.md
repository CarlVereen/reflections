# 🎬 Training video scripts — Service Pro CRM

I can't record video, but here are tight, ready-to-record scripts (screen-record
with Loom or your phone). Each is short on purpose — buyers watch short videos.
You can also hand these to a freelancer or an AI voice tool.

---

## Video 1 — "Set up in 5 minutes" (≈2:30)

> **[Screen: the sheet, Start Here tab]**
> "Welcome to Service Pro CRM. In the next five minutes you'll have your whole
> business running from this one sheet. Let's go."
>
> **[Click ⚙️ Settings]**
> "First, Settings. Type your business name, your email, your phone, and — this
> one matters — your Google review link. I'll show you where to find that in a
> minute. Add your payment instructions too."
>
> **[Reload, show ⚡ CRM menu]**
> "Reload the page and you'll see this ⚡ CRM menu. Click Set up / rebuild CRM.
> The first time, Google asks you to authorize — that's just because it's YOUR
> copy in YOUR account. Click through Advanced, then Allow."
>
> **[Open Quick Actions panel]**
> "Now click Open Quick Actions panel. This is your daily command center. Add a
> lead right here — name, phone, service, value — and it's saved with a follow-up
> date already set. That's setup. You're live."

## Video 2 — "Your daily 2-minute routine" (≈1:45)

> **[Quick Actions panel]**
> "Every morning, open the panel. 'Follow-ups due' shows exactly who to call
> back today. A customer calls? Add them as a lead in five seconds, right here."
>
> **[Leads tab]**
> "In the Leads tab, drag a lead through the stages — New, Contacted, Quoted, Won.
> See how overdue follow-ups turn red? Nothing slips through the cracks anymore."
>
> **[Dashboard]**
> "And the Dashboard updates itself — leads this week, your pipeline value,
> revenue, win rate. You never build a report again."

## Video 3 — "Get paid + get reviews" (≈2:00)

> **[Jobs tab]**
> "When a job's done, mark it Done and Paid. The client's total spent updates
> automatically."
>
> **[Invoices tab → Create & email invoice]**
> "Need an invoice? Add a row, click it, then ⚡ CRM, Invoices, Create & email
> invoice. Boom — a branded PDF, emailed to your client, saved to your Drive."
>
> **[Automations → Send review requests]**
> "Here's the money-maker. After a paid job, click Send review requests. Every
> happy customer gets a friendly email with your Google review link. More
> reviews means more customers find you. Turn on daily automations and this,
> your follow-ups, and appointment reminders all run on autopilot."
