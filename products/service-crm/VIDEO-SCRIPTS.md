# 🎬 Training video scripts — Service Pro CRM

Tight, ready-to-record scripts for Loom / YouTube (or your phone). Each is short on purpose —
buyers watch short videos. Screen directions are in **[brackets]**; the spoken lines follow.
These match the current **app-first** build (Home / Clients / Jobs / Billing / More).

---

## Video 1 — "Set up in 10 minutes" (≈3:00)

> **[Screen: the Start-Here PDF / the copy link]**
> "Welcome to Service Pro CRM. In about ten minutes you'll have your whole service business
> running from a real app on your phone. Let's set it up — you only do this once."
>
> **[Click the copy link → File ▸ Make a copy]**
> "Open your link and click File, Make a copy. That's it — the entire app is now yours, private,
> in your own Google account. Nothing to install, nothing to paste."
>
> **[Reload → ⚡ CRM menu → Set up / rebuild database]**
> "Reload the sheet and you'll see this ⚡ CRM menu. Click Set up / rebuild database. The first
> time, Google asks you to authorize — that's just because it's YOUR copy in YOUR account. Click
> Advanced, then Go to Service Pro CRM, then Allow."
>
> **[Extensions ▸ Apps Script ▸ Project Settings ▸ Time zone]**
> "Quick but important: set your timezone here, so scheduled jobs land on your calendar at the
> right hour."
>
> **[Deploy ▸ New deployment ▸ Web app → Execute as Me, Only myself → Deploy → copy URL]**
> "Now turn on the app: Deploy, New deployment, pick Web app, set Execute as Me and access to
> Only myself, Deploy, and copy this link. That link is your app — open it, and on your phone,
> Add to Home Screen."
>
> **[In the app: More ▸ Business Settings]**
> "Last thing — open Business Settings in the app and fill in your name, phone, tax rate, review
> link, and payment link. You're live."

## Video 2 — "Your daily routine" (≈2:00)

> **[App: Home tab]**
> "This is your home screen. New leads, your open pipeline, jobs this week, revenue, win rate.
> Down here, Follow-ups due — exactly who to call back today."
>
> **[Tap + Lead / add a client]**
> "A customer calls? Tap + Lead, add their name, phone, and address — a follow-up date is set
> automatically."
>
> **[Clients tab → open a client → Call / Text / 🧭 Directions]**
> "Open any client and you can Call or Text them right here — or tap Directions to get Google
> Maps directions straight to their door. Handy when you're headed to a job."
>
> **[Jobs ▸ + New job → set date + time]**
> "Scheduling a job? Pick the client and service, set a date and a time. Watch this —"
>
> **[Switch to Google Calendar, show the event]**
> "— it shows up automatically on your Google Calendar, with the client's address as the
> location. Reschedule in the app and the calendar updates itself. No double entry."

## Video 3 — "Estimate → paid, and get reviews" (≈2:15)

> **[App: client card → Create estimate → add line items]**
> "From a client, tap Create estimate and add your services. Prices auto-fill from your price
> book — and you can override any line. Tax is added for you."
>
> **[Send estimate]**
> "Send it, and the client gets a clean, branded PDF estimate."
>
> **[Open estimate → Approve → invoice + job → set date + time]**
> "When they say yes, tap Approve. It creates a draft invoice from the quote AND schedules the
> job — it even asks for the date and time, which syncs to your calendar."
>
> **[Invoice → Mark paid]**
> "Send the invoice, and when you're paid, tap Mark paid. The client's lifetime spend updates
> automatically."
>
> **[Sheet: ⚡ CRM ▸ Automations ▸ Send review requests + Turn ON daily autopilot]**
> "Here's the money-maker. After a job's done, Send review requests — every happy client gets a
> friendly email with your Google review link. Turn on daily autopilot and your review requests,
> follow-up reminders, appointment reminders, and overdue flags all run by themselves."

## Recording tips
- Record on a **fresh copy** with a little demo data so the dashboard looks alive.
- Use a **phone-width** window for the app portions (it's a mobile-first app).
- Keep each video **under 3 minutes**; link all three from your listing.

## Thumbnail / title ideas
- "Run your service business from a $69 app (no monthly fees)"
- "The CRM that auto-syncs jobs to your Google Calendar"
- "Set up your whole CRM in 10 minutes — full walkthrough"
