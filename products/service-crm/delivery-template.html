Set up in ~10 minutes (on a computer)

  1. Open the link above and click File ▸ Make a copy. The whole CRM is now yours, private, in your own Google Drive. (All the code comes with the copy; nothing to paste.)
  2. Reload the sheet. A ⚡ CRM menu appears at the top. Click ⚡ CRM ▸ Set up / rebuild database, then approve the one-time Google permission (see the note below; the warning screen is normal).
  3. Set your timezone so scheduled jobs land on your calendar at the right time: Extensions ▸ Apps Script ▸ Project Settings (⚙️) ▸ Time zone → pick your zone.
  4. Turn on your app: Extensions ▸ Apps Script, then Deploy ▸ New deployment ▸ (gear) Web app. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Only myself, click Deploy, and Authorize. Copy the Web app URL it gives you.
  5. Open that URL. This is your app. On your phone, use your browser menu to Add to Home Screen. Fill in Settings, then open More ▸ Price book and add your services and prices (they auto-fill your quotes).
  6. Back in the sheet, ⚡ CRM ▸ Automations ▸ Turn ON daily autopilot for automatic follow-ups, appointment reminders, and review requests.

Seeing “Google hasn’t verified this app”? That’s normal. Because everything runs inside your own Google account, Google shows a warning during setup. Click Advanced → Go to Service Pro CRM (unsafe) → Allow. It’s your own copy; your data never leaves your Drive. (Work/school account with no “Advanced” link? Use a personal @gmail.com account.)
Opening the app on a computer: if you get a Google Drive “unable to open the file” error, you’re signed into more than one Google account. Open the app link in an Incognito window signed into just the account that owns the sheet (or make it your default account). Full deploy help is in the included guide.
🛡️ Your data & privacy: everything stays inside your own Google account: your customer data is never sent to any AI or third party, and no one else (not even us) can see it. Keep your sheet private; don’t share it “anyone with the link.” Full guidance is in the included guide.
