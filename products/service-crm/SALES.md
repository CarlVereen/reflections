## Full description

**Stop losing jobs to a messy notes app and forgotten follow-ups.**

Service Pro CRM turns a single Google Sheet into a real customer manager built for
people who *do the work* — cleaners, landscapers, detailers, contractors, trainers,
handymen, pool & HVAC techs, movers, and more.

**What's inside:**
- 📊 **Live dashboard** — new leads this week, open pipeline value, jobs this week,
  revenue this month, win rate, and lifetime revenue. Updates on its own.
- 🎯 **Lead pipeline** — color-coded New → Contacted → Quoted → Won/Lost, with
  follow-up dates that turn **red the moment they're overdue** so nothing slips.
- 🔔 **Automatic follow-up emails** — get a morning list of who to call back.
- 🗓️ **Job scheduler** — track what's booked, done, and paid.
- 👥 **Client book** — total spent per client calculates automatically.
- 💵 **Invoice log** — Draft / Sent / Paid / Overdue at a glance.

**Why it beats a $30/month CRM app:**
- ✅ One-time price. Own it forever.
- ✅ Runs in *your* Google account — your data never leaves your Drive.
- ✅ Works on phone and laptop, wherever Google Sheets works.
- ✅ Nothing to install. Copy it and go.

**How it works:** After purchase you get a link. Click *File ▸ Make a copy* and it's
yours — private, editable, ready. A one-page guide walks you through the first 5 minutes.
