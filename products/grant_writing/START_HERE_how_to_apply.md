# START HERE — How to actually apply (step by step)
### Howard's Orchard LLC · plain-English walkthrough

New to all this? That's normal. Do them in this order. #1 and #2 take under an
hour total. Don't try to do everything at once — just start at the top.

---

## ✅ #1 — Amber Grant (do this first — Ashley applies)
**What:** $10,000, given to a woman-owned business. Ashley is 51% owner, so she's
the applicant. You can re-apply every month.
**Where:** **https://ambergrantsforwomen.com/get-an-amber-grant/apply-now/**

**Exact steps:**
1. Open that link. Click the **"Apply Now"** button.
2. Fill in **Ashley's** info and the business info (Howard's Orchard LLC,
   Catalina AZ, agriculture / U-pick orchard).
3. When it asks you to **tell your story and how you'd use the money**, paste the
   essay from `amber_grant_essay.md` (read it first, tweak to sound like you).
4. Pay the **$15 application fee** by credit card. (One $15 fee covers all three
   monthly grant categories. Can't afford it? Email
   **info@ambergrantsforwomen.com** to ask for a fee waiver.)
5. Click **Apply Now / Submit.** Done.

**Good to know:** No business plan or pitch deck needed. Any stage is fine — even
$0 revenue. Winners are announced by the **21st of the following month.** If you
don't win, **apply again next month** — persistence pays here.

**Where:** **hiddenstar.org** → look for "Galaxy Grants" / "Apply."
**Steps:** Open the form, paste the ready-made answers from
`galaxy_grant_entry.md`, submit before **July 31.** Takes minutes.

## ✅ #3 — Sign up on two free grant platforms (5 min each, then they find grants FOR you)
- **Hello Alice** — **helloalice.com** → create a free account, fill in your
  business profile. They'll email you matching small-business + ag grants.
- **IFundWomen** — **ifundwomen.com** → fill out the "universal application"
  once; they notify you when women's grants open that fit.

*(These don't pay out today — they're how you catch future grants without hunting.)*

---

## ✅ #4 — The water-system money (a phone call, not a website)
**What:** NRCS **EQIP** pays 50–90% of irrigation/water upgrades. This is the
biggest dollar item, and it starts with a **phone call**, not an online form.
**Steps:** Follow `nrcs_call_script.md`. First find your office at
**farmers.gov/service-center-locator** (enter 85739), then call and use the script.

---

## ✅ #5 — Organic cost-share (cheap, do before Dec 31)
**What:** Get 75% (up to $750) of organic-certification costs reimbursed by the
**Arizona Department of Agriculture.**
**Where:** **agriculture.az.gov** → search "Organic Certification Cost Share," or
call **Robert Nick, (602) 542-0439.** Ask him to send you the application + AZ
W-9. *(This one reimburses you after you get certified — ask him how to start
certification too.)*

### The golden rules for all of them
1. **Only say what's true.** Honest applications keep you in good standing.
2. **Keep copies** of everything you submit (screenshots or PDFs) — put them in a
   folder so we can reuse the answers next time.
3. **Tell me what happens** after each one — especially the NRCS call — and I'll
   help with the next step.
4. **Losing is normal.** Grants are a numbers game. Amber alone you can enter
   every single month.
