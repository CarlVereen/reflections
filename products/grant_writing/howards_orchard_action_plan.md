## TIER 2 — Arizona / USDA local (highest fit — start the conversations now)

### ⭐ NRCS EQIP — funds your water system (the best fit you have)
The Environmental Quality Incentives Program pays a big share of irrigation
efficiency + water-management upgrades: **drip conversion, soil-moisture
sensors, water-harvesting, pipelines.** Pays **50–75% of cost — up to 90% for
socially disadvantaged producers (women qualify → Ashley).** Continuous signup.
- **Action:** call the **Tucson/Pima NRCS field office**, ask for a (free)
  conservation plan for irrigation efficiency + water harvesting. That plan is
  the doorway to funding, and it can also support your permaculture goals and an
  **Organic Initiative** transition. ([EQIP AZ](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/programs-initiatives/environmental-quality-incentives-program/arizona/environmental-quality))

### USDA Organic Certification Cost Share (OCCSP) — via AZ Dept of Ag
Reimburses **75% (up to $750/yr)** of organic-certification costs. You already
farm organically — certifying is cheap here and unlocks premium pricing + more
grants. **Deadline Dec 31, 2026.** Contact: Robert Nick, (602) 542-0439.
([AZDA OCCSP](https://agriculture.az.gov/plantsproduce/organic-certification-cost-share-program-occsp))

## TIER 3 — Bigger money, needs lead time (we prep now, apply when open)

### USDA Value-Added Producer Grant (VAPG) — the big one for jams/cider/pies
- Planning grant **up to $75,000**; working-capital grant **up to $250,000**.
- **You get PRIORITY:** VAPG **reserves a share of funds for Beginning Farmers
  and Socially Disadvantaged farmers — you qualify as BOTH.** That's a real edge
  over typical applicants.
- Jams & jellies (current) + cider, pies, dried fruit (planned) = textbook VAPG.
- **Match reality (honest):** VAPG needs a **1:1 match**. At ~$8k/yr sales,
  matching a $75k ask isn't realistic — so we'll **right-size the request** to a
  modest **planning grant** with a match you can actually meet (cash + eligible
  in-kind). Better to win a $15–25k planning grant than lose a $75k swing.
- FY26 window closed; **we use the lead time to build the required feasibility
  study + business/marketing plan — I draft both.**
  ([VAPG AZ](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/business-programs/value-added-producer-grants-18))

### Western SARE — Farmer/Rancher grant (~$25–30k)
Funds on-farm research. Perfect for **trialing elderberry & mulberry in the
desert** and **water-efficient permaculture** — turns your experiments into
funded projects.

### REAP (Rural Energy for America) — if we add solar/cold storage
Catalina is rural (well under 50k pop), and you're an ag producer, so a
**solar-powered irrigation pump** or **cold storage** could be grant-funded
($1,500–$500k). Pairs naturally with the water project.

### Also on the radar
- **FMPP / LFPP** (Farmers Market & Local Food Promotion) — for the U-pick /
  agritourism / direct-to-consumer side.
- **AZ Specialty Crop Block Grant** — mostly funds industry-wide/association/
  research projects, not single farms directly; lower direct fit, next cycle
  ~March 2027. Possible via a research or partnership angle.
- **FSA Microloans / Beginning Farmer programs** — financing + higher grant
  rates if the family's ownership makes you "beginning farmers" (<10 yrs).
