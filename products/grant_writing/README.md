## The one-paragraph summary
Howard's Orchard is a 45-year-old, woman-owned (Ashley 51%), pesticide-free,
beginning-farmer (2022) family U-pick orchard — a profile that qualifies for
**agriculture, women's, beginning-farmer, value-added, and water-conservation**
grants that most for-profit businesses can't touch. The plan: apply to the
recurring small grants every month (Amber, Freed, Pollination, Verizon), knock
out the one-time ones (Galaxy, NASE, NRCS water, organic cost-share), and build
the big applications (VAPG, Business Builder $100k, AgWest $15k) off one shared
farm story and set of financials. Keep everything true, keep copies, keep applying.
