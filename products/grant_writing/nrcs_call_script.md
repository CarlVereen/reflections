# NRCS Phone Call Script — Water System / EQIP
### Howard's Orchard LLC · the single highest-dollar call on the plan

**Goal of this call:** get on NRCS's radar for **EQIP** funding for irrigation
efficiency + water harvesting, and start a (free) conservation plan. You are a
**Beginning Farmer** and a **historically underserved / woman-owned** producer —
say both, because they unlock the **90% cost-share** and **up to 50% advance
payments.**

---

## Before you call (5 min of prep)
- **Find your local office:** go to **farmers.gov/service-center-locator**, enter
  85739 → it gives the **Pima County / Tucson USDA Service Center** (NRCS + FSA
  share the office). Call that NRCS number. (Or search "NRCS Arizona state
  office" and ask them to route you.)
- **Have handy:** farm address (4101 E Pinal St), ~1 acre in production / 220
  trees (expandable to 1.5), that you bought the farm fall 2021 (first season
  2022), Ashley owns 51%, and a one-line goal: *"convert to high-efficiency
  irrigation and add rainwater harvesting."*
- **Note:** to receive EQIP you must have an **FSA farm number** and be in the
  USDA system. If you don't have one yet, that's step one — ask about it (below).

---

## The opening line (say this)
> "Hi, my name is ___ and my wife Ashley and I own Howard's Orchard, a small
> U-pick fruit orchard in Catalina. We're **beginning farmers** — our first
> season was 2022 — and we want to improve our irrigation efficiency and add
> water harvesting. I'd like to start a conservation plan and find out about
> **EQIP** funding. Can you help me get started?"

## Five things to make sure you say
1. **"We're beginning farmers"** (first season 2022) — triggers priority + higher rates.
2. **"My wife owns 51% — we're a woman-owned / historically underserved farm."**
   — triggers the top cost-share rate and advance payments.
3. **"We farm organically, pesticide-free"** — ask about the **EQIP Organic Initiative.**
4. **"We're in a drought area and want to cut water use"** — irrigation efficiency
   and water conservation are top NRCS priorities in Arizona.
5. **"We'd like to add drip/micro-irrigation, soil-moisture sensors, and
   rainwater harvesting."** (Practices they'll know: *Irrigation System–
   Microirrigation 441, Irrigation Water Management 449, Water Harvesting Catchment.*)

## Six questions to ask them
1. **"What's the next EQIP ranking (batching) cutoff date I need to apply by?"**
2. **"Do I have an FSA farm number, or do I need to set one up first? How?"**
3. **"Can we schedule a site visit to build a conservation plan?"**
4. **"As beginning + historically underserved farmers, do we qualify for the
   higher payment rate and advance payments?"**
5. **"Which practices fit a small orchard converting to efficient irrigation +
   water harvesting?"**
6. **"Is there anything I should bring or prepare before the site visit?"**

---

## After the call — write down
- The name + direct line of the person you spoke with
- The **ranking/batching deadline** (this drives everything)
- Whether you need an FSA farm number, and how to get it
- The **site-visit date**
- Any forms they mentioned (e.g., **CPA-1200** EQIP application)

**Then tell me what they said** and I'll help you prep the application and the
site visit. This one plan can also open the door to organic transition help and
future water funding — it's the keystone.
