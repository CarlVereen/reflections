## ⭐ USDA Business Builder Award — up to $100,000 (the big AZ one)
- **What:** Grants from the **USDA Southwest Regional Food Business Center**,
  administered in Arizona via **Local First Arizona**, to build capacity of small
  and mid-sized farms and value-added food businesses. AZ awards range
  **$75,000–$100,000.**
- **Why you fit:** eligible applicants include **AZ agricultural producers and
  value-added producers**; "small-sized" = gross annual sales **under $350,000**
  (you're at ~$8k — easily qualifies). Priorities include increasing production
  capacity and infrastructure to connect local producers to markets — exactly
  your cider/dried-fruit/pie expansion.
- **How to apply:** check current status at **localfirstaz.com** and
  **goodfoodfinderaz.com**; join the AZ Dept of Ag grant-notification email list.
- **Action:** find out if a round is open now or when the next opens. This one is
  worth building a full application for (it overlaps heavily with the VAPG drafts
  we already have).
- Source: [Local First AZ / SW Regional Food Business Center](https://localfirstaz.com/news-blog/usda-southwest-regional-food-business-center-makes-grants-up-to-100000-available-for-arizona-farms-and-food-businesses)

## American Farmland Trust — Brighter Future Fund — up to $10,000
- **What:** Grants to **for-profit farmers** for equipment, supplies,
  infrastructure, financial planning, and professional services — including
  buying equipment to improve productivity or crop quality.
- **Why you fit:** for-profit US farm with **>$1,000 in ag sales** (yes). Perfect
  for dehydrators, a cider press, or irrigation gear.
- **Status:** 2025 cycle closed (awards early 2026); **next cycle expected 2026**
  — get on their list.
- Apply: [farmland.org/brighterfuture](https://farmland.org/brighterfuture/)

## FruitGuys Community Fund — up to $5,000
- **What:** Grants to **small U.S. farms (≤250 acres)** for **sustainability
  projects** — solar power, water systems, pollinator habitat, seed-saving.
- **Why you fit:** you're well under 250 acres and operating since 2022; your
  **water/permaculture and pollinator** goals are exactly what they fund. They've
  funded orchard/food-forest projects.
- **Status:** 2026 round closed Jan 30; **reopens ~late 2026 for the next year.**
- Apply: [fruitguyscommunityfund.org/apply](https://fruitguyscommunityfund.org/apply/)

## NASE Growth Grant — up to $4,000 (open year-round)
- **What:** The National Association for the Self-Employed gives members grants of
  up to $4,000 for **equipment, marketing, hiring, or expansion.**
- **Why you fit:** any self-employed member business; use it for value-added
  equipment or marketing.
- **Catch:** requires **NASE membership** (~$120/yr) — small cost, and members can
  apply year-round and re-apply.
- Apply: nase.org → Growth Grants

## Comcast RISE — $5,000 + marketing/tech package (CHECK service area)
- **What:** $5,000 monetary grant plus a marketing/technology makeover.
- **Why you might fit:** must be **51% woman- or minority-owned** (Ashley ✓) and
  **operating 3+ years** (yes, since 2022).
- **Catch to verify:** business must be in a **Comcast service area** — **Tucson
  is largely Cox territory**, so confirm eligibility before spending time.
- Apply: comcastrise.com
