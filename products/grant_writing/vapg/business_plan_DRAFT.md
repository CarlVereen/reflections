## 1. Business Description

Howard's Orchard LLC is a family-owned U-pick fruit orchard on ~1 acre (≈220
trees, expandable to 1.5) in Catalina, Pima County, Arizona. Founded by Jim
Howard in the 1980s as "a community project," the farm has operated for ~45 years
and has been owned since fall 2021 by Carl & Ashley Vereen (Ashley 51%). It grows
apples, peaches, pears, and nuts using pesticide-free / organic practices, keeps
ducks and chickens for eggs, and sells via U-pick July–November. The farm
currently produces small-batch jams and jellies.

**Legal structure:** LLC, woman-owned (51%), beginning farmer (first season 2022).

**Mission:** Keep a 45-year-old desert family farm alive and productive by growing
clean, pesticide-free food and sharing farm life with the community.

## 2. The Opportunity & Products

Expand the value-added line from jams/jellies into **pressed cider, dried fruit,
and pies** — shelf-stable, higher-margin products that (a) sell year-round beyond
the U-pick season, (b) monetize cull/surplus fruit currently wasted, and (c) sell
at premium prices to an existing, loyal customer base.

**Product roadmap:**
- **Now:** jams, jellies.
- **Phase 1 (highest margin / longest shelf life):** dried fruit, pressed cider.
- **Phase 2:** pies (needs licensed kitchen).
- **New crops:** elderberry, mulberry — climate-hardy, high-value for value-added
  (syrups, preserves) and market differentiation.

## 3. Market Analysis

- **Market:** Tucson metro (~1M people) within ~30–40 minutes; strong, growing
  demand for local/organic food.
- **Warm audience:** existing U-pick visitors — high-conversion, zero-cost buyers
  of branded take-home products.
- **Channels:** on-farm/U-pick point of sale → farmers markets → online/seasonal
  gifts → local specialty retail (wholesale, later).
- **Differentiation:** pesticide-free/organic, single-farm identity, 45-year
  local story, and the U-pick experience — none of which grocery brands can copy.

## 4. Marketing & Sales Strategy

1. **Point-of-experience sales:** display cider, dried fruit, jams, and pies at
   the U-pick checkout — impulse + gift purchases at premium prices.
2. **Brand & story:** lean into the Howard's legacy and family stewardship; get
   **organic certified** to headline "certified organic, pesticide-free."
3. **Farmers markets & local events:** Tucson-area weekend markets for reach
   beyond the farm.
4. **Online/seasonal:** dried fruit and cider ship well — holiday gift boxes.
5. **Email/social to the existing customer list** to drive off-season sales.
