## The honest fit assessment

- **Federal grants (Grants.gov): not for us.** The SBA and federal programs
  **do not grant startup money to for-profit businesses** — they fund nonprofits
  and governments. ([SBA](https://www.sba.gov/funding-programs/grants))
- **What a for-profit CAN win:** private/corporate small-business grants and
  pitch contests. Small ($1k–50k), competitive, but real — and some are free/
  fast to enter, so expected value is positive even at low odds.
- **The big money needs a nonprofit.** The rich, winnable streams (faith-based,
  community-services) require a **501(c)(3)**. That's a strategic fork (below),
  and those same streams are exactly what we'll tap for our **church clients.**

## The strategic fork (decide soon, not today)

**Do we stay for-profit, or add a nonprofit?**

- **For-profit (simplest start):** we earn **fees** from churches. Grants to
  ourselves are a minor bonus (the contests above). Fastest to launch.
- **Nonprofit / social enterprise (bigger grant access):** if we frame the
  venture as *"helping under-resourced churches and community groups access
  funding they'd otherwise miss,"* a 501(c)(3) could unlock the faith-based and
  community-capacity grant streams (e.g. NJ SE2D, Enterprise FBDI) — the same
  streams we'll write for clients. More setup, more paperwork, more upside.

**My recommendation:** start **for-profit and earn fees now** (don't let
paperwork stall the first dollar). Revisit the nonprofit option once we've
landed a few church clients and know it's worth it. Keep the faith-based grant
list — it's gold for the **clients**, even if not for us.

## What this proved about the tool

Running case-zero surfaced the exact things our service must get right, and
which the pipeline already handles:
- **Eligibility filtering matters most** — half of all "grants" don't apply to a
  given applicant (for-profit vs nonprofit, demographic, geography).
- **Deadlines drive everything** — the urgent-but-eligible ones are the wins.
- **The funder's own page is the source of truth** — always verify before applying.
