## 1. Project title
**"From Orchard to Pantry: Building Year-Round Value-Added Capacity at a 45-Year-Old
Arizona Family Farm."**

## 2. Executive summary
Howard's Orchard will build a small on-farm value-added processing operation —
licensed kitchen, cider pressing, fruit drying, and packaging — that converts our
pesticide-free apples, peaches, and pears (including cull fruit now wasted) into
cider, dried fruit, jams, and pies sold year-round. Grant funds build the
production capacity and the market channels (farm store, farmers markets, online,
and local wholesale) to connect our products — and eventually other small local
growers' fruit — to Tucson-area consumers. The project moves a seasonal U-pick
farm to a resilient, year-round local food business and anchors a value-added
outlet for the region's small orchardists.

## 3. Statement of need
- A one-acre orchard sells only July–November; income is seasonal and capped.
- A significant share of good fruit (bruised/undersized "seconds") is unsellable
  as U-pick and **currently wasted** — lost income and lost food.
- Southern Arizona's small orchardists lack nearby value-added processing to turn
  surplus fruit into sellable product.
- Desert water costs and scarcity threaten small-farm viability.
