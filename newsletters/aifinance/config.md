## Identity
- **newsletter_id:** `aifinance`
- **display name:** The Vertical (working title) — *AI for Accounting & Finance Pros*
- **one-line promise:** The weekly, no-hype briefing on the AI tools actually reshaping
  accounting and finance work — what launched, who funded it, and whether it's worth your
  firm's time.

## Reader avatar
- **Who:** Accounting-firm partners and managers, controllers, fractional/outsourced CFOs,
  and finance-ops leaders at SMB-to-mid-market companies. Practitioners, not engineers.
- **What they need:** To keep up with a fast-moving tool landscape without drowning in
  generic AI hype — a trusted filter that says what's real, what's production-ready, what's
  still a demo, and what it means for close, tax, audit, AP/AR, and FP&A workflows.
- **What keeps them up at night:** Falling behind competitors who automate; buying the
  wrong tool; accuracy/compliance risk (PCAOB, AICPA); how to reskill their team.
- **Where they gather:** LinkedIn (accounting-influencer threads), r/Accounting, AICPA &
  CIMA communities, CPA Practice Advisor / Accounting Today readerships, firm-of-the-future
  and QuickBooks ProAdvisor circles, Going Concern.

## Voice & tone rules
- **Practitioner-to-practitioner, not vendor-to-prospect.** Skeptical by default; assume
  the reader has seen the hype and wants the "so what."
- **Plain-English, numerate.** Explain the workflow impact, not the model architecture.
- **Show the receipts.** Every claim links to a primary source (launch post, funding
  announcement, changelog, filing, standard). No unsourced "studies show."
- **Name the maturity honestly:** label items as *shipping / pilot / demo / vaporware*.
- **Affiliate honesty (hard rule):** any tool link that is an affiliate link is disclosed
  inline, and is only ever a genuine recommendation the editorial gate would pass anyway.
- **No investment or tax advice.** Educational framing; "verify with your own judgment /
  professional standards." Do not tell readers how to file.
- **No em dashes as a crutch, no breathless AI-doomer or AI-utopian framing.** Measured.

## Content pillars (3–5)
1. **Tool Radar** — new launches, major feature/changelog updates, and honest hands-on
   maturity calls (shipping vs. demo) for close, tax, audit, AP/AR, and FP&A tools.
2. **The Money** — funding rounds, M&A, and valuations in vertical AI accounting; what the
   capital signals about where the category is heading.
3. **Inside the Firms** — how real firms (Big Four down to solo practices) are deploying
   AI: public case studies, agent rollouts, measured results, and what broke.
4. **Standards & Guardrails** — PCAOB/AICPA/regulatory guidance, documentation
   requirements, accuracy/audit-trail risk, and governance for AI-assisted work.
5. **Skills & Playbooks** — practical prompt packs, workflow templates, and reskilling —
   the seedbed for digital products.

## Money model
- **Primary: Affiliate + digital products.** AI/SaaS tools in this space pay generous,
  often recurring commissions (commonly 20–30%, some 40–60%). Digital products =
  close/tax/audit playbooks and prompt packs built from top-performing issues.
- **Secondary: Premium tier / lead-gen** to AI-implementation consultants.
- **Status: OFF until interest is proven.** No affiliate links, no product pitches, no
  premium wall until the thresholds below are met over 2–3 issues. Even then, affiliate
  placements must pass the editorial/quality gate as genuine, disclosed recommendations.
- **Rationale:** weakest *direct subscription* WTP evidence of the five niches; monetize
  via affiliate/products first (see strategy/niches.md #4, strategy/monetization.md row 4).

## Proven-interest thresholds (defaults from .claude/agents/nl-analyst.md)
Treat interest as **proven** only when, sustained over ~2–3 issues, ALL hold:
- an engaged base: **≥ ~300–500 subscribers**, AND
- healthy engagement: **open rate ≥ ~35%, click rate ≥ ~3%**, AND
- **organic growth**: net-positive signups without paid push, AND
- **qualitative demand**: replies/requests, or rising archive-search traffic.

*Defaults, not proven-optimal — the Analyst recalibrates as real data arrives and records
any change here.*
