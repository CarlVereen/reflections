## 1. What we sell (the one-liner)

> We turn a local business's online presence into a lead-generation machine: a website built as a funnel, a reputation that pulls people in, and weekly data that proves it's working — all for one monthly fee.

We are **not** a website builder. We are the person who helps established businesses **generate leads**. The website is just how the leads get delivered.

---

## 2. The four pillars (one subscription)

1. **Conversion Website** — Not a brochure. A funnel. "Your best salesperson, working 24/7, doing the same thing every time." Every page moves the visitor toward becoming a customer. SEO (top of Google) + AEO (top of AI answer results) so they get found in the first place.
2. **Reputation Management** — Respond to reviews + post regularly on **Google *or* Yelp** (whichever platform the business gets found on first) so the profile stays alive and trusted.
3. **Review & Referral Growth** — Proactive outreach to *generate* new reviews and referrals. (The pillar most competitors skip — we don't just protect reputation, we grow it.)
4. **Weekly Data Reports** — Clicks, funnel entry, conversion rate. Decisions from data, not "I feel."

**The through-line:** Reputation drives people to the site → the site converts them → the data makes it convert better. It compounds.

## 4. Pricing

| Tier | Price | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| **Founding rate** | **$189/mo** | First 5 clients only. Locked for 12 months. In exchange for a testimonial + case study. |
| **Standard rate** | **$497/mo** | Everyone after the founding cohort. |

**Why these numbers:**
- **Charm pricing** (the left-digit effect — Anderson & Simester field research): prices ending in 9 outperform round numbers. In the agency/direct-response world, **$497** (ending in 7) is the standard monthly-retainer convention. $189 already ends in 9.
- **Founding rate is a deliberate loss-leader** to get proof cheaply and lower friction — not the real price.
- **A $20–40k/mo business can easily afford $497/mo** for a lead system. Pricing at $200 forever would (a) require ~25 clients to hit $4k and (b) signal "cheap = low value" to established owners.

**The $4k math:**

| Price | Clients needed for ~$4k profit |
|---|---|
| $189/mo (founding) | ~22–27 |
| **$497/mo (standard)** | **~9** |

Plan: land the first 5 at $189 to prove it works → everyone after pays $497 → hit $4k with **~9 clients**.

## 5. Delivery & operations

- **We host the website ourselves** (not on the client's FTP). This:
  - keeps the AI auto-updates clean (we control the environment),
  - shrinks our admin footprint (we only need their **Google/Yelp profile** access),
  - creates natural lock-in: **cancel = the site goes dark.**
- **Access we need from the client:** their Google Business Profile *or* Yelp page. That's it.
- **Delivery model for responses:** AI-drafted → **Carl approves** → posted. Nothing goes live without sign-off.
