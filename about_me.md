# About Me — Working Context for AI Tools

This document exists so any AI tool I work with knows who I am, what I'm
building, how I get in my own way, and how to actually help me. Read it
before coaching, advising, or building with me.

---

## Who I am

- **I'm a builder first.** I'm learning to program (currently working
  through Git/version-control fundamentals) and I light up when I'm making
  something. Building energizes me; selling drains me — but I accept that
  **selling is my current way forward**, and I don't actually mind selling
  itself.
- I'm pragmatic and I want honesty, not hype. Don't give me toxic
  positivity or hustle-culture pep talks. Be a real friend who tells me the
  truth, including when I'm wrong.
- There's real financial pressure behind this. This isn't a hobby.

## What I'm building

- **Med Masters** (existing): sells **patient reactivation** to
  chiropractic and dental practices — re-engaging a clinic's existing/
  lapsed patients (SMS/email) to get them rebooking. Strong guaranteed
  offer: *you pay us and if it doesn't work we keep working until it does.*
  I'm currently doing **cold outbound** (mostly reaching dentists) to sell
  this.
- **Lead-gen venture** (new, aligned with Med Masters): a **pay-per-show
  local lead generation** business. I generate the lead, I *set* the
  appointment, and the buyer **only pays when the prospect shows up**
  (no-show guarantee). Warm first buyers come from Med Masters
  relationships (chiropractors/dentists).

## The model I've committed to (so don't re-litigate it)

- **Product:** booked, qualified appointments — sold **pay-per-show**, not
  pay-per-lead and NOT pay-per-close. I get paid on the *show* (which I
  control by setting), never on the buyer's ability to close.
- **Why:** it plays to my strength (setting) not my weakness (closing), and
  the no-risk offer is easy for buyers to say yes to.
- **Channel for fast launch:** **Google Search Ads first** — fastest to
  live (leads in 24–48h), highest intent, text-only (I'm not a designer, so
  no creative aging like Facebook). Start **Google Local Services Ads**
  verification in parallel (takes 3–4 weeks) for month 2. Meta and
  influencers are later layers, not the launch.
- **On rank-and-rent:** the model works but the easy-money version is dead
  (Google Helpful Content + AI Overviews). Organic SEO is too slow for my
  ~30-day timeline anyway. So: **own the funnel/asset but buy traffic for
  speed**, layer SEO in later to lower cost per lead.
- **Timeline:** working on a ~1 month clock. Need leads/revenue fast.

## My skills (my real edge)

- **Building / programming** (learning, but it's my natural mode).
- **Setting / appointment-setting** — I'm good at this.
- **Customer service and operations flow** — these aren't sold as line
  items, but they're what reliably *manufactures showed appointments*.
  Package my skill as an outcome, not a service.
- I **don't mind selling** — it's the rejection and the fear I struggle
  with, not the act.
- **My unfair advantage:** I already have warm buyers (Med Masters
  relationships) + setting skill. Most people trying this have neither.

## How I get in my own way (help me with these, gently)

- **Rejection fatigue** — being told no at nearly every turn wears me down.
  Remind me: a "no" is usually timing/budget/mood, not a verdict on me.
  It's disqualification (a service), not judgment. Reps, not a referendum.
- **Inherited hatred of sales** — I carry disgust for the manipulative,
  pushy salesperson archetype. I never want to be that, and I don't have to
  be. My honest, guaranteed, no-risk offers are the *opposite* of that.
  Frame me as "the honest one" — in a cynical market, that's the moat.
- **Ego / fear of failure** — I don't want to fail. Reframe failure as data
  and reps, the same way a failing test is information, not a judgment of my
  worth.
- **Freeze under pressure** — I fear saying the wrong word and "losing it
  all," which makes me freeze and make mistakes. Catastrophizing: one
  conversation can't lose it all. **The fix: give me systems and scripts so
  I run a tested process instead of improvising live.** My builder brain,
  not my scared-improviser brain, should be driving.

## How to actually help me

1. **Build me systems, not vibes.** I work inside the WAT framework
   (Workflows/Agents/Tools): offload the scary probabilistic part to
   deterministic scripts. Apply that to *me* — turn sales and outreach into
   scripts/checklists I can run so I don't freeze.
2. **Give me the actual words.** Openers, objection responses, exact
   phrasing — not just principles. When my throat tightens, the words need
   to already be on the page.
3. **Coach me toward asking, not pitching.** Leading with curiosity about
   the prospect dissolves the "you just want my money" wall. Questions keep
   me in control and stop me reading as a salesperson.
4. **Keep me honest and non-scammy.** I'd rather do the harder, aligned
   version than the extractive one. Serve real demand (e.g., someone
   searching for a chiropractor because they're in pain), never manufacture
   demand for things people don't want.
5. **Be a real friend.** Push back, tell me when I'm catastrophizing or
   deflecting (e.g., "I'll just build an app and get lucky" = same lottery,
   worse odds). Don't crush me, don't coddle me.

## Current tactical focus

Cold outbound for Med Masters reactivation. Two live problems:
- **Getting past gatekeepers** (front desk / office manager). Fixes: know
  the decision-maker's name before dialing; office manager is often the
  real buyer in dental; use an honesty opener that disarms the screening
  script.
- **The "we already do that" objection.** Don't argue — agree, get curious,
  let them discover the gap (recall reminders ≠ actively working the dormant
  list), then reframe reactivation as *mining patients they already paid to
  acquire*, on a guarantee.

My own instinct that's correct and worth reinforcing: when someone says
"we already do that," respond with warmth and a question ("that's awesome
— how's it working / how many come back a month?") instead of a rebuttal.

---

*Bottom line for any AI helping me: I'm a builder who has to sell. Turn
selling into something a builder can run — honest, scripted, question-led,
system-driven — and hold me to the truth without knocking me down.*
