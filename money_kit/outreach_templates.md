# Outreach Templates (to get clients directly)

Fiverr/Upwork brings inbound. These get you OUTbound clients faster — often
your first $250 comes from direct outreach, not a marketplace.

Use `tools/outreach_drafter.py` to mass-personalize any of these. Columns
expected in your CSV: `first_name`, `company`, `pain_point`.

---
## Cold email — local small business
Subject: quick idea for {company}

Hi {first_name},

I help small businesses clean up the boring admin stuff — messy contact lists,
meeting notes, follow-up emails — so you get hours back each week.

If you've got a spreadsheet that's a mess or meetings you never write up, I can
take that off your plate fast (and cheap). Worth a 10-minute call?

— Carl

---
## DM — Facebook/LinkedIn group for a niche
Hey {first_name}! I saw you run {company}. I do quick-turnaround admin
automation for small businesses — lead list cleanup, meeting notes, outreach
emails. First small job is half price so you can see if it's useful. Want me to
take something off your plate this week?

---
## Warm follow-up (no reply after 3 days)
Hi {first_name}, just floating this back up — I know inboxes get buried. Even a
"not right now" helps me. If admin chaos is a someday problem, I'll be here. 🙂

---
## After you deliver (ask for review + referral)
Glad that helped, {first_name}! Two quick favors if you're happy:
1) A short review really helps me out.
2) Know one other business owner drowning in admin? I'll give them the same
   first-job-half-price deal.

---
## Where to find prospects (free)
- Local Facebook business groups (search "[your city] small business")
- LinkedIn — search a niche + "owner" (dentist, realtor, gym, salon)
- Google Maps — local businesses with websites but obvious admin gaps
- Nextdoor / local subreddits — "recommendations" threads

**Target a single niche first.** "I help dentists" beats "I help everyone."
Tell me which niche and I'll build you the list-ready CSV + custom templates.
