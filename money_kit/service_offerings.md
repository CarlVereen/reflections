# Service Offerings & Pricing

Keep prices simple and flat. Small businesses want a number, not an estimate.

## 1. Lead List Cleanup — $75
**What they get:** A messy CSV/spreadsheet of contacts returned clean:
deduplicated, standardized phone numbers, validated emails, invalid rows flagged.
**Delivery:** Same day. **Tool:** `tools/clean_lead_list.py`
**Upsell:** Monthly maintenance for $50/mo.

## 2. Meeting Notes Write-Up — $75 per meeting
**What they get:** Send a recording or transcript → get back clean Markdown/Doc
with summary, decisions, and an action-item table (owners + status).
**Delivery:** Same day. **Tool:** `tools/transcript_to_actions.py` (+ my edit pass)
**Upsell:** Weekly recurring "I take notes for all your meetings" — $200/mo.

## 3. Personalized Outreach Pack — $125 for 50 emails
**What they get:** 50 individually personalized cold/warm emails from their lead
list, ready to send. **Tool:** `tools/outreach_drafter.py`
**Upsell:** Full campaign management — $300/mo.

## 4. ⭐ Starter Automation Bundle — $250 (your headline offer)
Everything above, scoped to one client:
- Clean their lead list (up to 500 rows)
- Write 50 outreach emails
- Set up a simple meeting-notes workflow they can reuse
**Why it works:** One sale = your subscription paid. Frame it as
"I'll get your admin chaos under control in one week."

## Pricing rules
- Always quote flat, never hourly (you're fast because of automation — don't punish that).
- Take 50% upfront for anything over $100.
- Offer a money-back guarantee on the first deliverable. Removes their risk, costs you nothing if your work is good.
