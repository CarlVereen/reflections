## The revenue logic (why this beats ads for a small, ad-free operation)

Ads pay pennies per subscriber and require huge lists. These models pay **dollars to
thousands of dollars** per buyer and work at small list sizes:

- **Lead-gen / services (newsletters 1, 3):** one client can be worth thousands. Viable
  with only hundreds of engaged readers. Slowest to set up, highest ceiling.
- **Subscriptions (newsletters 2, 5):** recurring MRR; needs a few % of free readers to
  convert. Compounds as the free list grows.
- **Digital products (all):** highest margin; first-dollar path at tiny list sizes;
  evergreen once built.
- **Affiliate (newsletter 4, secondary everywhere):** works from day one, no product to
  build; capped upside and must stay honest/disclosed.

## Sequencing from zero (per newsletter)

1. **Weeks 1–8:** publish + grow; ship a lead magnet; place *only* honest, disclosed
   affiliate links where genuinely useful. First revenue usually here (affiliate/product).
2. **Once ~a few hundred engaged readers:** launch the first **digital product** built
   from the best-performing issues.
3. **Once the free list converts predictably:** turn on the **primary model** (paid tier,
   or the services/lead-gen funnel).
4. **Ongoing:** track revenue-per-offer (workflow 40); cut what doesn't convert.

## What we will NOT do

- No display/programmatic ads (your constraint).
- No undisclosed sponsorships or affiliate links.
- No selling a product we haven't vetted through the same fact/quality gates as editorial.
- No fabricated testimonials, subscriber counts, or revenue claims.
