# Google Business Profile — Experts & Resources Reference

**Purpose:** The people, communities, and playbooks that are the real curriculum for the GBP/reputation half of our review→referral business. Follow these to learn the craft, partner for hard cases (e.g. suspensions), and study how the top of the market packages and prices this work.

---

## The official Product Experts program (authoritative source)

Google Business Profile "Product Experts" are an official, tiered volunteer program: **Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond.** They answer questions and escalate cases inside the GBP Help Community. The badge is *earned* through forum contribution — you can't buy it.

- **Master directory (every Product Expert, filterable):** https://productexperts.withgoogle.com/directory
- **"Meet our Product Experts" (GBP-specific roster):** https://support.google.com/business/community-guide/289980627/meet-our-product-experts
- **GBP Help Community (post questions; PEs respond, and can escalate):** https://support.google.com/business/community
- **What the program is:** https://support.google.com/communities/answer/9138806

## The industry heavyweights (follow regardless of badge)

- **Joy Hawkins** — founder of **Sterling Sky** and the **Local Search Forum**; the most-cited authority on GBP. Her team publishes the canonical suspension/reinstatement playbook.
- **Darren Shaw / Whitespark** — runs the annual **Local Search Ranking Factors** study (the definitive "what actually moves local rankings" survey of experts).
- **BrightLocal** — publishes the annual **Local Consumer Review Survey** (the stat source: % who read reviews, star thresholds, etc.) plus expert GBP guides.

## How we use this list

1. **Learn the craft** — these blogs/forums are our curriculum for the GBP + reputation side. Start with the Sterling Sky suspension playbook and the Whitespark ranking-factors study.
2. **Partner for hard cases** — GBP suspensions/reinstatements need real Product-Expert-level skill. Rather than learn it cold, we can partner with or refer to a PE (like cnomo) for that piece and keep it as a premium service tier.
3. **Study the top of the market** — Sterling Sky, Steady Demand, Uberall show how GBP work is packaged and priced at the high end.
4. **Reality check** — the space is crowded (per cnomo). Our wedge stays: review→referral loop + done-for-you for a *specific high-ticket niche*, not "another review tool."
